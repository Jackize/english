# C2 Writing — IELTS Academic 8.5–9.0

**Level:** C2 (CEFR) · **IELTS Academic:** 8.5–9.0 · **Số unit:** 5  
**Kỹ năng:** Task 1 precision, Task 2 thesis/counter-thesis, complex syntax, lexical sophistication, cohesion without visible connectors, timed mock  
**Ngôn ngữ:** hướng dẫn tiếng Việt; đề bài, bài mẫu và ngôn ngữ mục tiêu tiếng Anh

> Band 9 không phải band 8 cộng thêm từ hiếm. Đó là band 8 **bớt đi** sự phô diễn: lập luận không thừa, câu phức vẫn đọc được một lần, và mỗi từ mang trọng lượng. Ở C2, tiêu chí cao nhất là **kiểm soát**: kiểm soát nhịp câu, sắc thái, mức độ khẳng định và cái giá của một lựa chọn từ.

### 0.1 Hồ sơ người viết C2

## Unit 1 — Task 1: Precision & Data Selection

**Mục tiêu:** Chọn 4–6 số liệu có chức năng; overview nêu quan hệ; tính số dẫn xuất chính xác; không liệt kê.  
**Sản phẩm:** 180–200 từ · **Thời gian:** 20 phút.

### 1.1 Đề bài

> The table below shows the share of household expenditure spent on four categories by two income groups in 1995 and 2025.
>
> Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

| Category | Low-income 1995 | Low-income 2025 | High-income 1995 | High-income 2025 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Housing | 32% | 41% | 18% | 24% |
| Food | 38% | 27% | 15% | 11% |
| Transport | 12% | 14% | 20% | 19% |
| Leisure | 8% | 10% | 27% | 33% |
| Other | 10% | 8% | 20% | 13% |

### 1.2 Model answer

> The table compares the proportion of household expenditure allocated to five categories by low- and high-income groups in 1995 and 2025.
>
> Overall, both groups spent more on housing and less on food, but they diverged in leisure: high-income households shifted towards it, while low-income households increased it only marginally. Housing became the largest category for the former and leisure for the latter, so the structure of expenditure reflected income rather than a common pattern of rising affluence.
>
> For low-income households, housing rose from 32% to 41%, overtaking food, which fell from 38% to 27%. Transport and leisure edged up by two percentage points each, while the “other” share declined slightly. The high-income group followed a similar pattern on housing and food: housing rose from 18% to 24%, while food fell from 15% to 11%. Its most distinctive shift, however, was towards leisure.
>
> The sharpest divergence was in leisure. High-income households spent 27% on it in 1995 and 33% in 2025, whereas low-income households spent only 8% and 10% respectively. By 2025, the high-income leisure share was more than three times the low-income share, a gap that had widened from 19 to 23 percentage points.

*≈194 từ.*

### 1.3 Phân tích

| Lựa chọn | Vì sao |
|---|---|
| Bỏ “other” ở phần thân | Không tạo ra so sánh mới; đã có 4 nhóm đủ |
| Dùng *overtaking food* | Cho thấy quan hệ thay đổi thứ hạng, không chỉ hai con số |
| Tính *three times* và *19 → 23 points* | Số dẫn xuất có chức năng, không trang trí |
| Overview nêu cả điểm chung (housing/food) và điểm phân kỳ (leisure) | Nêu quan hệ giữa hai nhóm, không chỉ “both changed” |
| Không giải thích nguyên nhân | Task 1 không yêu cầu; giải thích sẽ lạc |

**Câu overview yếu vs mạnh:**

| Yếu | Mạnh |
|---|---|
| *Overall, both groups spent money on housing, food, transport and leisure.* | *Overall, the two groups diverged: low-income households spent more on housing, high-income households more on leisure.* |
| *Housing increased for both groups.* | *Housing rose for both, but it became the largest category only for low-income households.* |

### 1.4 Language bank — precision

| Cụm | Chức năng | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| allocated to | được phân bổ cho | *the share allocated to housing* |
| devoted to | dành cho | *households devoted 41% to housing* |
| overtake | vượt qua | *Housing overtook food.* |
| edge up | tăng nhẹ | *Leisure edged up by two points.* |
| diverge / converge | phân kỳ / hội tụ | *The two groups diverged.* |
| the sharpest divergence | khác biệt rõ nhất | *The sharpest divergence was in leisure.* |
| a gap widened from X to Y | khoảng cách tăng | *The gap widened from 19 to 23 points.* |
| more than three times | hơn ba lần | *...more than three times the low-income share* |
| respectively | theo thứ tự | *8% and 10% respectively* |
| in structural terms | về mặt cấu trúc | *In structural terms, the budgets differed.* |
| a higher proportion of | tỉ lệ cao hơn | *...a higher proportion of expenditure* |
| the former / the latter | cái trước / cái sau | *the former... the latter...* |

### 1.5 Guided practice

**1. Tính số dẫn xuất.**

- High-income leisure 2025: 33%; low-income leisure 2025: 10%. → **3.3 times** / **more than three times**.
- Housing gap 1995: 32 − 18 = **14 points**. 2025: 41 − 24 = **17 points**.
- Food share fall, low-income: 38 → 27 = **11 points**.

**2. Viết lại câu không liệt kê.**

> *Housing was 32% for low-income and 18% for high-income in 1995. In 2025, it was 41% and 24%.*

**Gợi ý:** *Housing absorbed a far larger share of low-income budgets in both years, and the gap widened from 14 to 17 percentage points.*

**3. Chọn overview đúng.**

- A. The table shows five categories and two income groups.
- B. Overall, the two groups diverged: housing dominated low-income budgets, while leisure dominated high-income budgets.
- C. Food fell for both groups.

**Đáp án:** B. A chỉ mô tả hình thức; C chỉ là một chi tiết.

**4. Sửa lỗi số liệu.**

| Sai | Đúng |
|---|---|
| *High-income leisure was three times higher than low-income leisure.* | *...more than three times the low-income share.* |
| *Housing increased from 32% to 41% for low-income, and 18% to 24%.* | *...and from 18% to 24% for high-income.* |
| *The percentage of housing was 41%.* | *The share of expenditure devoted to housing was 41%.* |

> The table shows the percentage of electricity generated from four sources in three countries in 2024.

| Source | Country A | Country B | Country C |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Coal | 45% | 20% | 5% |
| Gas | 25% | 35% | 15% |
| Nuclear | 15% | 10% | 40% |
| Renewables | 15% | 35% | 40% |

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Overview nêu quan hệ giữa ba nước.
- [ ] Chọn 4–6 số liệu có chức năng.
- [ ] Có ít nhất 2 số dẫn xuất.
- [ ] Không liệt kê cả 12 ô.
- [ ] Không giải thích nguyên nhân.

### 1.7 Common mistakes

| ❌ Sai | ✅ Đúng | Mã | Vì sao |
|---|---|---|---|
| *The table shows about expenditure.* | *The table shows expenditure.* | PREP | *Show* không cần *about*. |
| *Housing was increased.* | *Housing rose / increased.* | S/V | *Increase* nội động từ. |
| *Three times higher than* | *Three times as high as* / *three times the figure* | COMP | *Times higher* gây tranh cãi. |
| *The percentage of housing was 41%.* | *The share devoted to housing was 41%.* | COL | *Percentage of housing* mơ hồ. |

## Unit 2 — Task 2: Thesis & Counter-Thesis

**Mục tiêu:** Viết thesis có điều kiện; trình bày counter-thesis mạnh nhất; phản bác vào giả định; kết luận không thêm ý.  
**Sản phẩm:** 300–320 từ · **Thời gian:** 40 phút.

### 2.1 Đề bài

> Some people argue that inherited wealth should be heavily taxed in order to preserve equality of opportunity. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

### 2.2 Model answer

> Inheritance is the point at which the market’s claim to reward effort collides with the family’s claim to dispose of its property. I agree that inherited wealth should be taxed more heavily than it is in most countries, but only as part of a broader regime that taxes capital consistently and uses the revenue to widen opportunity.
>
> The case for taxation begins with the asymmetry it corrects. A person who inherits a large estate acquires advantages that were never earned and cannot be matched by a person who begins with nothing. If opportunity is to depend on talent and effort, the transmission of unearned advantage across generations is precisely what should be constrained. The objection that parents have a right to provide for their children is real, but it is a right to support, not a right to reproduce advantage indefinitely.
>
> The strongest counter-argument is that inheritance taxes are inefficient and evadeable. Wealthy families can move assets, establish trusts or insure against the tax, so the burden may fall on those with less sophisticated advice. This is a serious objection, but it establishes only that badly designed taxes fail, not that the objective is misguided. The remedy is a broad base with few exemptions, consistent valuation and international cooperation, not abandonment of the principle.
>
> A second objection concerns double taxation: wealth may already have been taxed as income. Yet capital gains often escape full taxation, and inherited assets can grow for decades without being taxed at all. The relevant question is not whether a particular pound has been taxed once, but whether the overall system treats earned income and inherited capital consistently.
>
> In conclusion, I agree that inherited wealth should be taxed more heavily, provided that the tax is broad, transparent and used to fund opportunity. The alternative — treating inheritance as morally neutral while pretending that opportunity is equal — is a more subtle form of privilege.

*≈316 từ.*

### 2.3 Phân tích — thesis/counter-thesis

**Thesis (câu 2):**

> *I agree that inherited wealth should be taxed more heavily..., but only as part of a broader regime that taxes capital consistently and uses the revenue to widen opportunity, rather than as a symbolic penalty on good fortune.*

Ba thành phần:

1. **Mức độ:** *agree* (không phải *strongly agree*).
2. **Điều kiện:** *only as part of a broader regime*.
3. **Phản đề bị loại:** *rather than as a symbolic penalty*.

**Counter-thesis (đoạn 3):**

> *The strongest counter-argument is that inheritance taxes are inefficient and evadeable.*

Đây là phiên bản mạnh nhất, không phải người rơm. Tác giả thừa nhận *“This is a serious objection”* rồi giới hạn: *“it establishes only that badly designed taxes fail”*. Đây là **phản bác vào phạm vi**, không phủ nhận sự thật.

**Câu chốt đoạn 4:**

> *The relevant question is not whether a particular pound has been taxed once, but whether the overall system treats earned income and inherited capital consistently.*

Câu này chuyển tiêu chí đánh giá từ “đã bị đánh thuế chưa” sang “hệ thống có nhất quán không” — kỹ thuật C2.

| Cụm | Chức năng | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| collides with | va chạm với | *...collides with the family’s claim.* |
| only as part of | chỉ như một phần của | *...only as part of a broader regime.* |
| rather than | thay vì | *...rather than a symbolic penalty.* |
| the asymmetry it corrects | sự bất đối xứng nó điều chỉnh | *The case begins with the asymmetry.* |
| unearned advantage | lợi thế không do công sức | *the transmission of unearned advantage* |
| reproduce advantage | tái tạo lợi thế | *...reproduce advantage indefinitely.* |
| the strongest counter-argument | phản biện mạnh nhất | *The strongest counter-argument is...* |
| this establishes only that | điều này chỉ chứng minh rằng | *This establishes only that bad taxes fail.* |
| the remedy is | giải pháp là | *The remedy is a broad base.* |
| double taxation | đánh thuế hai lần | *The objection concerns double taxation.* |
| the relevant question is not whether X but whether Y | câu hỏi không phải X mà là Y | *The relevant question is not whether... but whether...* |
| provided that | với điều kiện | *...provided that the tax is broad.* |
| morally neutral | trung lập về đạo đức | *treating inheritance as morally neutral* |
| a subtle form of privilege | một dạng đặc quyền tinh vi | *...a more subtle form of privilege.* |

### 2.5 Guided practice

**1. Viết thesis có điều kiện cho đề:**

> *Governments should make university education free for all students.*

**Gợi ý:** *I support free tuition in principle, but only if it is funded from general taxation and accompanied by measures to maintain course quality; otherwise it risks transferring costs to students in other forms.*

**2. Phân loại câu.**

| Câu | Thesis / Counter-thesis / Refutation |
|---|---|
| *The strongest objection is that free tuition benefits richer families disproportionately.* | |
| *This objection identifies a real design flaw, but it does not defeat the principle.* | |
| *I agree that tuition should be free, provided that access is genuinely equal.* | |

**Đáp án:** Counter-thesis · Refutation · Thesis.

**3. Tránh người rơm.**

| Người rơm | Sửa |
|---|---|
| *Opponents simply do not care about poor students.* | *Opponents argue that free tuition subsidises families who could afford to pay.* |
| *This policy is obviously unfair.* | *This policy imposes costs on those who receive little benefit.* |

**4. Sửa lỗi logic.**

| Sai | Đúng |
|---|---|
| *Inheritance tax is good because inheritance is bad.* | *Inheritance tax is justified because it limits the transmission of unearned advantage.* |
| *All rich people avoid tax, so the tax cannot work.* | *Some wealthy families can avoid the tax, which is an argument for a broader base and international cooperation.* |

## Unit 3 — Discussion: Complex Syntax & Nuance

**Mục tiêu:** Duy trì câu phức có kiểm soát; phân biệt *growth* với *meaning*; dùng concession và điều kiện; tránh nhị phân.  
**Sản phẩm:** 300–320 từ · **Thời gian:** 40 phút.

### 3.1 Đề bài

> Some people believe that the pursuit of economic growth is incompatible with a meaningful life. Others argue that growth is a precondition for any meaningful life.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

### 3.2 Model answer

> The claim that growth and meaning are incompatible has an intuitive appeal: a society that measures itself by GDP may indeed be rich in goods and poor in time. Yet the opposite claim — that growth is a precondition for meaning — is equally too simple. The relationship depends on what growth consists of, how its proceeds are distributed, and whether institutions leave room for goods that markets do not price.
>
> The case against growth begins with the observation that beyond a certain threshold, income buys less well-being than one might expect. Once basic needs are met, the quality of work, the strength of relationships and the availability of uncommodified time matter more than further consumption. Growth can thus be self-defeating when it erodes the conditions that make life worthwhile.
>
> The case for growth is strongest where material scarcity is still acute. In low-income countries, growth funds sanitation, education and healthcare; without it, meaning is pursued under conditions of avoidable suffering. Even in rich countries, growth can finance the transition to cleaner energy and the care economy, neither of which is cheap. The error is not growth but the assumption that all growth is equivalent: a wind farm and a luxury tower both raise GDP, yet they contribute very differently to human welfare.
>
> The reconciliation lies in distinguishing growth from its composition. A society can pursue growth in clean energy, public health, education and care, while restraining growth in activities that impose hidden costs. This requires institutions capable of valuing what markets omit — time, health, community, ecological stability — and of resisting the temptation to treat GDP as a summary of everything that matters.
>
> In conclusion, growth is neither inherently meaningful nor inherently corrosive. It is a means whose value depends on what it is used for and who benefits. The task is not to choose between growth and meaning, but to build an economy in which the two are not systematically opposed.

*≈320 từ.*

### 3.3 Phân tích — câu phức có kiểm soát

**1. Câu mở đầu có ba mệnh đề nhưng vẫn đọc được:**

> *The claim that growth and meaning are incompatible has an intuitive appeal: a society that measures itself by GDP may indeed be rich in goods and poor in time.*

- Mệnh đề chính: *The claim... has an intuitive appeal*.
- Mệnh đề quan hệ: *that growth and meaning are incompatible*.
- Mệnh đề giải thích sau dấu hai chấm: *a society that measures itself by GDP...*.
- Tương phản trong cụm: *rich in goods and poor in time*.

**2. Nominalisation có chức năng, không lạm dụng:**

| Động từ | Danh từ hóa | Câu |
|---|---|---|
| *consume* | *consumption* | *Further consumption matters less than...* |
| *distribute* | *distribution* | *...how its proceeds are distributed* |
| *compose* | *composition* | *...distinguishing growth from its composition* |

**3. Concession hai chiều:**

> *The case against growth begins with... Growth can thus be self-defeating... The case for growth is strongest where... The error is not growth itself but the assumption...*

Tác giả không bênh một phía; chuyển tiêu chí từ “growth good/bad” sang “composition”.

**4. Tránh “both sides have merits”:**

| Yếu | Mạnh |
|---|---|
| *Both views have advantages and disadvantages.* | *The relationship depends on what growth consists of and how its proceeds are distributed.* |
| *I think both are important.* | *Growth is a means whose value depends on what it is used for.* |

### 3.4 Language bank — growth, meaning, evaluation

| Cụm | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| beyond a certain threshold | vượt một ngưỡng nhất định | *Beyond a certain threshold, income buys less well-being.* |
| uncommodified time | thời gian không bị thương mại hóa | *...the availability of uncommodified time* |
| self-defeating | tự làm suy yếu mục tiêu | *Growth can be self-defeating.* |
| erode the conditions | bào mòn điều kiện | *...erodes the conditions that make life worthwhile.* |
| material scarcity | sự thiếu thốn vật chất | *...where material scarcity is acute.* |
| avoidable suffering | đau khổ có thể tránh được | *...conditions of avoidable suffering.* |
| the care economy | nền kinh tế chăm sóc | *...finance the care economy.* |
| all growth is equivalent | mọi tăng trưởng như nhau | *The error is assuming that all growth is equivalent.* |
| the composition of growth | cơ cấu tăng trưởng | *...the composition of growth.* |
| hidden costs | chi phí ẩn | *...activities that impose hidden costs.* |
| value what markets omit | định giá điều thị trường bỏ qua | *Institutions capable of valuing what markets omit.* |
| systematically opposed | đối lập một cách hệ thống | *...not systematically opposed.* |

### 3.5 Guided practice

**1. Gộp ba câu thành một câu phức, giữ nghĩa.**

> *Cities build more roads. Commutes may become shorter. Neighbourhoods may be destroyed.*

**Gợi ý:** *Although building more roads may shorten commutes, it can also destroy the neighbourhoods that make a city worth living in.*

**2. Chuyển sang nominalisation.**

> *People consume more, but they do not necessarily live better.*

**Gợi ý:** *Higher consumption does not necessarily translate into a better quality of life.*

**3. Sửa câu quá tải mệnh đề.**

> *Although growth is important because it funds education which is necessary for people who want to improve their lives, but it can also damage the environment which is a problem.*

**Gợi ý:** *Growth can fund education and improve lives, but it can also damage the environment.*

**4. Viết câu concession có giới hạn.**

Đề: *Economic growth should be the main priority of government.*

**Gợi ý:** *Growth deserves priority where material deprivation remains widespread; it does not follow that it should override every other consideration.*

> Some people think that technological progress has made life more meaningful. Others believe it has made life shallower.
>

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Không dùng *both sides have merits*.
- [ ] Có ít nhất 2 câu phức dài nhưng đọc được một lần.

|---|---|---|---|

## Unit 4 — Double Question: Register, Cohesion & Expertise

**Mục tiêu:** Trả lời hai câu hỏi liên kết; kiểm soát register; dùng cohesion không lộ từ nối; định nghĩa khái niệm trước khi lập luận.  
**Sản phẩm:** 300–320 từ · **Thời gian:** 40 phút.

### 4.1 Đề bài

> Why do some people distrust experts, and should experts have a greater role in democratic decision-making?

### 4.2 Model answer

> Distrust of expertise is often treated as a failure of public understanding. It is better understood as a response to three features of modern expert authority: its opacity, its record of error, and its entanglement with power. Whether experts should have a greater role in democratic decisions depends on which of these features a reform addresses.
>
> Opacity is the most obvious source of suspicion. Experts often speak in a register that excludes non-specialists, and the institutions that certify expertise — universities, professional bodies, regulators — are not always transparent about their funding or internal disagreements. The problem is not that expertise is false; it is that its authority is asserted rather than demonstrated.
>
> A record of error compounds the difficulty. Experts have been wrong about diets, financial risk and the pace of technological change. Yet the fact that experts can be wrong does not make expertise dispensable. The appropriate response is to institutionalise correction — open data, replication, adversarial review — rather than to treat every expert claim as equally contestable.
>
> The third feature, entanglement with power, is the most politically significant. Expertise is not produced in a vacuum; it is commissioned, funded and interpreted by actors with interests. This does not mean that all expert advice is corrupted, but it does mean that the question “who decided what counts as expertise?” is itself democratic.
>
> On that view, experts should have a greater role in informing decisions, but not in deciding them. Their contribution is to enlarge the range of consequences that citizens and representatives can see, not to substitute technical judgement for political judgement. Independent advisory bodies, published uncertainties and citizen panels can make expertise accountable without making it impotent.
>
> In conclusion, distrust is not irrational. It responds to real problems of opacity, error and power. The remedy is not less expertise but more accountable expertise — expertise that explains its limits and accepts that the final authority in a democracy must remain political.

*≈320 từ.*

### 4.3 Phân tích — register và cohesion

**1. Register học thuật, không hành chính:**

| Quá trang trọng/khô | Tự nhiên hơn |
|---|---|
| *It is imperative to ascertain the veracity of expert claims.* | *Citizens need to know whether expert claims are reliable.* |
| *The aforementioned phenomenon...* | *This distrust...* |
| *Utilise* | *use* |

**2. Cohesion không lộ từ nối:**

Đoạn 2 bắt đầu bằng *Opacity is the most obvious source of suspicion.* Đoạn 3 tiếp bằng *A record of error compounds the difficulty.* Đoạn 4 tiếp bằng *The third feature, entanglement with power...*. Ba đoạn được nối bằng **cấu trúc liệt kê ngầm**, không cần *Firstly/Secondly/Thirdly*.

**3. Câu chuyển tiêu chí:**

> *Whether experts should have a greater role in democratic decisions depends on which of these features a reform addresses.*

Câu này trả lời câu hỏi thứ hai bằng cách đặt điều kiện, không đưa ra “yes/no” đơn giản.

**4. Câu kết có chức năng:**

> *...expertise that explains its limits and accepts that the final authority in a democracy must remain political.*

Không lặp lại *in conclusion, experts are important*. Nó đưa ra tiêu chí cuối: authority phải ở chính trị, không ở kỹ thuật.

### 4.4 Language bank — expertise, democracy, register

| Cụm | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| a failure of public understanding | sự thiếu hiểu biết của công chúng | *Distrust is often treated as a failure of public understanding.* |
| entanglement with power | sự dính líu với quyền lực | *...its entanglement with power.* |
| opacity | tính không minh bạch | *Opacity is a source of suspicion.* |
| certify expertise | chứng nhận chuyên môn | *Institutions that certify expertise.* |
| assert authority | khẳng định thẩm quyền | *Its authority is asserted rather than demonstrated.* |
| compound the difficulty | làm khó thêm | *A record of error compounds the difficulty.* |
| captured regulators | cơ quan bị chi phối | *...or captured regulators.* |
| institutionalise correction | thể chế hóa việc sửa sai | *...institutionalise correction.* |

### 4.5 Guided practice

**1. Trả lời hai câu hỏi trong một thesis.**

Đề: *Why do people move to cities, and is this trend desirable?*

**Gợi ý:** *People move to cities mainly for work and services, but whether the trend is desirable depends on whether cities can absorb them without sacrificing the conditions that made them attractive.*

**2. Sửa register.**

| Quá khô/trang trọng | Tự nhiên |
|---|---|
| *It is incumbent upon policymakers to ameliorate the situation.* | *Policymakers need to improve the situation.* |
| *The populace is sceptical of expert pronouncements.* | *The public is sceptical of expert claims.* |
| *We must endeavour to ascertain...* | *We need to establish...* |

**3. Nối đoạn không dùng “Firstly/Secondly”.**

Viết câu mở đầu cho ba đoạn về *cost, access, quality*.

**Gợi ý:** *Cost is the most visible barrier. Access is a subtler one. Quality is harder still to measure.*

**4. Viết câu kết không lặp.**

Đề: *Should voting be compulsory?*

**Gợi ý:** *Compulsory voting may strengthen participation, but it cannot by itself make participation meaningful; that depends on whether citizens have reasons to believe their vote matters.*

### 5.2 Task 1

> The bar chart shows the percentage of companies in four sectors that reported using artificial intelligence (AI) in 2020 and 2025.
>
> Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

| Sector | 2020 | 2025 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Finance | 38% | 72% |
| Manufacturing | 22% | 58% |
| Retail | 15% | 47% |
| Healthcare | 9% | 35% |

#### Model answer

> The bar chart compares the proportion of companies using artificial intelligence in four sectors in 2020 and 2025.
>
> Overall, adoption rose in every sector, but the gap between finance and healthcare widened. Finance remained the most intensive user, while healthcare recorded the fastest relative growth from a low base. By 2025, the range had shifted upwards rather than narrowed: all four sectors exceeded 30%, yet the difference between the highest and lowest remained 37 percentage points.
>
> Finance led in both years, with adoption rising from 38% to 72%. Manufacturing recorded the largest absolute increase, from 22% to 58%, while retail grew from 15% to 47%. Both more than doubled over the period, and healthcare almost quadrupled, from 9% to 35%, yet it remained the least intensive sector. Relative growth was therefore fastest where adoption had been lowest, but it was not enough to close the gap with finance.
>
> The data therefore shows diffusion rather than convergence. AI became more common across the economy, but its adoption continued to reflect sectoral differences in resources, data availability and the returns to automation.

*≈180 từ.*

#### Phân tích nhanh

- **Overview nêu quan hệ:** *diffusion rather than convergence*.
- **Số dẫn xuất:** *more than doubled*, *almost quadrupled*, *37 points*.
- **Mỗi cặp số có chức năng:** finance làm mốc dẫn đầu; manufacturing/retail so sánh tốc độ; healthcare làm đáy của khoảng cách.
- **Không giải thích nguyên nhân** trong phần thân; câu cuối chỉ nhận định nhẹ về *sectoral differences*, vẫn trong phạm vi dữ liệu.

### 5.3 Task 2

> Some people believe that governments should regulate artificial intelligence strictly, even if this slows innovation. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

#### Model answer

> The regulation of artificial intelligence is debated as a trade-off between safety and innovation. I agree that strict regulation is justified for applications that affect rights, safety and access to essential services, but the case for regulating research itself is weaker. The relevant distinction is not between “strict” and “light” regulation, but between rules that target conduct and rules that attempt to control a general-purpose technology.
>
> The case for strict rules is strongest where AI is deployed in decisions that individuals cannot easily contest. A recruitment algorithm that excludes candidates, a credit model that denies a loan, or a diagnostic system that recommends treatment can cause serious harm while remaining opaque to the person affected. In such cases, the market cannot discipline harm that is diffuse, technical and invisible to the affected party. Regulation can require explanation, audit and a right of appeal.
>
> The counter-argument is that strict rules may entrench incumbents. Compliance is expensive, and large firms can absorb costs that startups cannot. This is a genuine risk, but it supports proportional rules — testing requirements for high-risk uses, exemptions for research, shared auditing infrastructure — rather than the absence of rules. The choice is not between innovation and protection; it is between forms of protection that preserve competition and forms that do not.
>
> A second objection concerns speed. Technology changes faster than legislation, so rules may be obsolete before they are enforced. Yet this argues for principles and institutions that can adapt, not for paralysis. A regulator with powers to investigate, disclose and revise is more useful than a statute that attempts to predict every application.
>
> In conclusion, strict regulation is justified where AI exercises power over individuals, provided that it targets conduct rather than research and is designed to remain contestable. The alternative — trusting the market to police a technology whose harms are invisible to its users — is not a defence of innovation but a refusal to govern it.

*≈320 từ.*

#### Phân tích nhanh

- **Thesis có phạm vi:** ủng hộ strict regulation cho *applications*, không cho *research*.
- **Counter-thesis:** compliance costs entrench incumbents.
- **Phản bác:** proportional rules, không bỏ luật.
- **Câu chốt:** *not a defence of innovation but a refusal to govern it* — đánh giá mạnh nhưng có lý.
- **Cohesion:** *in such cases, this is a genuine risk, yet, a second objection, in conclusion* — có từ nối nhưng không dày đặc.
