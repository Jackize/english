# B1 Writing — IELTS Academic Foundation

**Level:** B1 (CEFR) · **IELTS:** khoảng 4.5–5.5 · **Số unit:** 8  
**Kỹ năng:** IELTS Academic Writing Task 1 (150+ từ) và Task 2 (250+ từ)  
**Ngôn ngữ:** hướng dẫn tiếng Việt; đề bài, bài mẫu và ngôn ngữ mục tiêu tiếng Anh

> Đây là level đầu tiên viết **Task 1 và Task 2 đầy đủ**. Mục tiêu là xây cấu trúc đúng, chọn số liệu hợp lý, viết đoạn mạch lạc và kiểm soát lỗi cơ bản. Chưa cần band 7; cần **đúng format, rõ ý, ít lỗi cản nghĩa**.

---

## 0. Giới thiệu & cách học

### 0.1 Sau 8 unit, bạn có thể

- Viết Task 1 đủ 150–180 từ với introduction, overview và 2 body paragraphs.
- Mô tả line graph, bar chart, pie chart, table, process và map ở mức B1.
- Viết Task 2 đủ 250–280 từ với thesis, 2 body paragraphs và conclusion.
- Dùng ngôn ngữ so sánh, xu hướng, nguyên nhân–kết quả, concession đơn giản.
- Tự sửa 4 nhóm lỗi: mạo từ, số ít/số nhiều, thì, mệnh đề.

### 0.2 Cách học một unit

1. **Đọc đề, gạch yêu cầu (3 phút).**
2. **Đọc model, gạch cấu trúc (5 phút).**
3. **Làm guided practice (10 phút).**
4. **Viết bài độc lập (25–35 phút).**
5. **Dùng checklist tự sửa (5 phút).**
6. **So với sample answer, ghi 3 lỗi (5 phút).**

### 0.3 Quy tắc

- Task 1: **không đưa ý kiến**, không giải thích nguyên nhân ngoài biểu đồ.
- Task 2: **trả lời đúng câu hỏi**, có thesis rõ.
- Không học thuộc bài mẫu; học **khung câu** và **cách triển khai ý**.
- Mỗi bài chỉ sửa 3–5 lỗi quan trọng nhất.
- Không dùng từ quá khó nếu không kiểm soát được.

### 0.4 Bản đồ 8 unit

| Unit | Dạng bài | Sản phẩm |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Task 1 structure + line graph | 150–170 từ |
| 2 | Bar chart | 150–170 từ |
| 3 | Pie chart + table | 150–170 từ |
| 4 | Process + map | 150–170 từ |
| 5 | Task 2 structure + opinion | 250–270 từ |
| 6 | Discussion essay | 250–270 từ |
| 7 | Problem–solution essay | 250–270 từ |
| 8 | Mock test | Task 1 + Task 2 |

### 0.5 Correction code

| Mã | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| S/V | Chia động từ | *It show* → *It shows* |
| T | Thì | *In 2010, it rise* → *In 2010, it rose* |
| ART | Mạo từ | *the increase of 10%* → *an increase of 10%* |
| PL | Số ít/số nhiều | *many people is* → *many people are* |
| PREP | Giới từ | *in 2010 to 2020* → *from 2010 to 2020* |
| WO | Trật tự từ | *I very like* → *I really like* |
| WF | Từ loại | *a significance increase* → *a significant increase* |
| COH | Liên kết | câu rời → thêm *while, whereas, because* |
| COMP | So sánh | *more higher* → *much higher* |
| P | Dấu câu | *However it* → *However, it* |

## Unit 1 — Task 1 Structure + Line Graph

**Mục tiêu:** Viết Task 1 đúng 4 phần; mô tả xu hướng line graph; có overview rõ.  
**Sản phẩm:** 150–170 từ.  
**Thời gian:** 35 phút.

### 1.1 Cấu trúc Task 1

1. **Introduction** — paraphrase đề.
2. **Overview** — 1–2 câu nêu xu hướng/khác biệt chính.
3. **Body 1** — nhóm dữ liệu thứ nhất.
4. **Body 2** — nhóm dữ liệu thứ hai.

Không cần conclusion. Không đưa ý kiến.

### 1.2 Đề bài mẫu

> The line graph shows the percentage of households with internet access in three countries between 2000 and 2020.

**Dữ liệu (fictional):**

| Year | Country A | Country B | Country C |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2000 | 20% | 10% | 5% |
| 2010 | 50% | 35% | 15% |
| 2020 | 85% | 70% | 40% |

### 1.3 Model answer

> The line graph compares the proportion of households with internet access in three countries from 2000 to 2020. Overall, internet access increased significantly in all three countries, and Country A remained the highest throughout the period.
>
> In 2000, only 20% of households in Country A had internet access, compared with 10% in Country B and 5% in Country C. Over the next ten years, the figure for Country A rose sharply to 50%, while Country B increased to 35%. Country C grew more slowly, reaching 15% in 2010.
>
> Between 2010 and 2020, all three countries continued to see growth. Country A reached 85%, and Country B rose to 70%. Country C saw the most dramatic relative change, climbing from 15% to 40%. By 2020, the gap between Country A and Country C had widened, although access in Country C was still eight times higher than in 2000.

*156 từ.*

### 1.4 Phân tích

- **Intro** paraphrase: *shows → compares*, *percentage → proportion*, *between 2000 and 2020 → from 2000 to 2020*.
- **Overview** nêu xu hướng chung + đặc điểm nổi bật (A cao nhất).
- **Body 1** nhóm 2000–2010; **Body 2** nhóm 2010–2020.
- Có so sánh, không chỉ liệt kê.
- Không đưa lý do tại sao internet tăng.

### 1.5 Language bank — trends

| Tăng | Giảm | Ổn định | Mạnh |
|---|---|---|---|
| rise | fall | remain stable | sharply |
| increase | decrease | stay constant | significantly |
| grow | decline | level off | steadily |
| climb | drop | plateau | gradually |

**Cấu trúc:**

- `X rose from 20% to 50%.`
- `There was a sharp rise in X.`
- `X saw a significant increase.`
- `X reached 85% in 2020.`
- `X remained the highest throughout the period.`
- `X was twice as high as Y.`

### 1.6 Guided practice

**1. Viết lại intro.**

> The chart shows the number of people who used public transport in three cities from 2005 to 2015.

**Gợi ý:** The bar chart compares public transport use in three cities over a ten-year period from 2005 to 2015.

**2. Chọn overview đúng.**

- A. In 2005, City A had 20,000 users.
- B. Overall, public transport use increased in all three cities, with City A remaining the most popular.
- C. The chart shows three cities.

**Đáp án:** B.

**3. Sắp xếp câu thành đoạn mô tả.**

> a. By 2015, it had reached 60%.  
> b. In 2005, Country B had 10% access.  
> c. The figure rose steadily over the next ten years.  
> d. Country B was the second highest.

**Đáp án:** b → c → a → d.

### 1.7 Write

**Đề bài:** The line graph shows the number of visitors to three museums in a city from 2010 to 2020.

| Year | Museum A | Museum B | Museum C |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2010 | 20,000 | 15,000 | 8,000 |
| 2015 | 30,000 | 18,000 | 12,000 |
| 2020 | 45,000 | 25,000 | 20,000 |

Viết 150–170 từ.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Intro paraphrase.
- [ ] Overview 1–2 câu, không số liệu vụn.
- [ ] Body chia theo thời gian hoặc đối tượng.
- [ ] Có so sánh.
- [ ] Đúng đơn vị.
- [ ] 150–170 từ.

### 1.8 Sample answer

> The line graph compares visitor numbers at three museums in a city between 2010 and 2020. Overall, all three museums attracted more visitors over the period, and Museum A remained the most popular.
>
> In 2010, Museum A received 20,000 visitors, while Museum B had 15,000 and Museum C had 8,000. Over the next five years, Museum A’s visitor numbers rose to 30,000, and Museum B increased to 18,000. Museum C also grew, reaching 12,000 in 2015.
>
> Between 2015 and 2020, the upward trend continued. Museum A saw the largest increase, climbing to 45,000 visitors, which was almost twice the figure for Museum B. Museum B reached 25,000, and Museum C rose to 20,000. By 2020, Museum C had closed the gap with Museum B, while Museum A remained clearly ahead.

*148 từ.*

### 1.9 Common mistakes

| ❌ Sai | ✅ Đúng | Mã |
|---|---|---|
| The graph shows about the number of visitors. | The graph shows the number of visitors. | WO |
| It increased 20%. | It increased **by** 20%. | PREP |
| The percentage were risen. | The percentage **rose**. | S/V |
| More higher than | **much higher than** | COMP |

## Unit 2 — Bar Chart

**Mục tiêu:** So sánh các hạng mục; nhóm dữ liệu; viết overview có đặc điểm nổi bật.  
**Sản phẩm:** 150–170 từ.  
**Thời gian:** 35 phút.

### 2.1 Đề bài mẫu

> The bar chart shows the average number of hours per week that men and women in a country spent on four free-time activities in 2025.

**Dữ liệu (fictional):**

| Activity | Men | Women |
|---|---:|---:|
| Watching TV | 18 | 15 |
| Sport | 6 | 4 |
| Reading | 4 | 7 |
| Socialising | 8 | 10 |

### 2.2 Model answer

> The bar chart compares the average weekly hours that men and women spent on four leisure activities in 2025. Overall, watching TV was the most popular activity for both groups, while reading was the least common among men.
>
> Men spent 18 hours a week watching TV, compared with 15 hours for women. Socialising was the second most popular activity for women, at 10 hours, while men spent 8 hours on it. Women also spent more time reading than men, at 7 and 4 hours respectively.
>
> Sport showed the opposite pattern. Men spent 6 hours a week on sport, whereas women spent only 4 hours. The difference between the two groups was smallest for socialising and largest for reading, where women spent almost twice as much time as men. In general, men spent more time on TV and sport, while women spent more time on reading and socialising.

*149 từ.*

### 2.3 Language bank — comparison

- `X was higher than Y.`
- `X was slightly higher than Y.`
- `X was nearly twice as high as Y.`
- `X was the most popular activity.`
- `X was the least common.`
- `The figure for X was similar to that for Y.`
- `By contrast, ...`
- `respectively` — theo thứ tự.

### 2.4 Guided practice

**1. Chọn từ đúng.**

1. Men spent 18 hours, **compared with / compared to** 15 hours for women. (both possible; *compared with* more common in IELTS)
2. Women spent **more / most** time reading than men.
3. Watching TV was the **most / more** popular activity.

**Đáp án:** 1. compared with · 2. more · 3. most

**2. Viết câu so sánh từ dữ liệu.**

- Men: 6 hours sport; women: 4 hours.
- Gợi ý: Men spent 6 hours a week on sport, whereas women spent only 4 hours.

### 2.5 Write

**Đề bài:** The bar chart shows the percentage of people in four age groups who used a gym at least once a week in 2025.

| Age group | Percentage |
|---|---:|
| 18–29 | 35% |
| 30–44 | 28% |
| 45–59 | 20% |
| 60+ | 12% |

Viết 150–170 từ.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Intro paraphrase.
- [ ] Overview nêu nhóm cao nhất/thấp nhất.
- [ ] Có ít nhất 2 câu so sánh.
- [ ] Không liệt kê từng số mà không nhận xét.
- [ ] 150–170 từ.

### 2.6 Sample answer

> The bar chart shows the proportion of people in four age groups who used a gym at least once a week in 2025. Overall, gym use was most common among younger people and declined steadily with age.
>
> The 18–29 group had the highest figure, at 35%. This was seven percentage points higher than the 30–44 group, where 28% of people used a gym regularly. The proportion then fell to 20% among people aged 45–59.
>
> The lowest figure was recorded for the 60+ group, at just 12%. This was almost three times lower than the figure for the youngest group. The difference between the two oldest groups was smaller, at eight percentage points. In general, the data shows a clear negative relationship between age and regular gym use, although the decline was gradual rather than sharp.

*147 từ.*

### 2.7 Common mistakes

| ❌ Sai | ✅ Đúng | Mã |
|---|---|---|
| The percentage of 18–29 is 35%. | The figure for the 18–29 group was 35%. | COH |
| It was more lower. | It was **much lower**. | COMP |
| 35% people | 35% **of** people | PREP |
| The chart shows the percentage of people used a gym. | ... people **who** used a gym. | S/V |

## Unit 3 — Pie Chart + Table

**Mục tiêu:** Mô tả tỉ lệ; dùng phân số; so sánh bảng; chọn dữ liệu nổi bật.  
**Sản phẩm:** 150–170 từ.  
**Thời gian:** 35 phút.

### 3.1 Đề bài mẫu

> The pie charts show how a household spent its monthly income in 2010 and 2025.

**Dữ liệu (fictional):**

| Category | 2010 | 2025 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Housing | 40% | 45% |
| Food | 25% | 15% |
| Transport | 15% | 15% |
| Other | 20% | 25% |

### 3.2 Model answer

> The pie charts compare the way a household spent its monthly income in 2010 and 2025. Overall, housing remained the largest expense, while the share spent on food fell significantly.
>
> In 2010, housing accounted for 40% of the household budget, followed by food at 25%. Transport made up 15%, and the remaining 20% was spent on other items. By 2025, the proportion spent on housing had risen to 45%, and the share of food had dropped to 15%.
>
> Transport remained unchanged at 15% in both years. The “other” category increased from 20% to 25%, which meant that it became the second largest expense by 2025. Overall, the household spent a larger share of its income on housing and other items, while food became less important in the budget.

*146 từ.*

### 3.3 Language bank — proportions

- `account for 40% of the total`
- `make up a quarter of ...`
- `represent one fifth of ...`
- `the largest/smallest share`
- `the remaining 20%`
- `just over/under half`
- `approximately two thirds`

### 3.4 Guided practice

**1. Chuyển phân số.**

1. 25% → a quarter
2. 20% → a fifth
3. 50% → half
4. 75% → three quarters

**2. Chọn động từ đúng.**

- Housing **accounted for / made up** 40% of the budget. (both)
- Food **accounted for / made up of** 25%. (accounted for / made up; not *made up of*)

**3. Viết câu so sánh 2010 và 2025.**

- Housing: 40% → 45%.
- Gợi ý: The share of housing rose from 40% to 45%.

### 3.5 Write

**Đề bài:** The table shows the average monthly spending of a family in 2020 and 2025.

| Category | 2020 | 2025 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Housing | $800 | $950 |
| Food | $400 | $450 |
| Transport | $200 | $250 |
| Entertainment | $100 | $150 |

Viết 150–170 từ.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Intro paraphrase.
- [ ] Overview nêu xu hướng chung.
- [ ] Có so sánh tổng/chi tiết.
- [ ] Dùng đúng đơn vị ($).
- [ ] 150–170 từ.

### 3.6 Sample answer

> The table compares the average monthly spending of a family on four categories in 2020 and 2025. Overall, spending increased in every category, with housing remaining the largest expense.
>
> In 2020, the family spent $800 a month on housing, $400 on food, $200 on transport and $100 on entertainment. By 2025, housing had risen to $950, an increase of $150. Food spending also grew, from $400 to $450, while transport rose from $200 to $250.
>
> The most dramatic relative change was in entertainment, which increased by 50%, from $100 to $150. Despite this, it remained the smallest category in both years. Housing accounted for the largest share of the budget, and the gap between housing and food widened slightly over the period. In general, the family’s spending rose steadily, but the basic cost of housing continued to dominate the budget.

*151 từ.*

### 3.7 Common mistakes

| ❌ Sai | ✅ Đúng | Mã |
|---|---|---|
| Housing was 40% of total. | Housing accounted for 40% of the total. | COH |
| Food made up of 25%. | Food made up 25%. | PREP |
| The percent of food was 15%. | The **percentage** of food was 15%. | WF |
| It rose from 40% at 45%. | It rose from 40% **to** 45%. | PREP |

## Unit 4 — Process + Map

**Mục tiêu:** Dùng bị động, từ nối thời gian, ngôn ngữ vị trí; mô tả quy trình/bản đồ.  
**Sản phẩm:** 150–170 từ.  
**Thời gian:** 35 phút.

### 4.1 Process

#### Đề bài mẫu

> The diagram shows how chocolate is made.

**Các bước (fictional):**

1. cocoa beans are harvested
2. beans are dried in the sun
3. beans are roasted
4. beans are ground into powder
5. powder is mixed with sugar and milk
6. mixture is heated and poured into moulds
7. chocolate is packed and delivered

#### Model answer

> The diagram illustrates the process of making chocolate. Overall, the process consists of seven main stages, from harvesting cocoa beans to delivering the finished product.
>
> First, cocoa beans are harvested by hand and then left to dry in the sun. After that, the beans are roasted at a high temperature. Next, they are ground into a fine powder. The powder is then mixed with sugar and milk to create a smooth mixture.
>
> In the following stage, the mixture is heated and poured into moulds. Once the chocolate has cooled and become solid, it is removed from the moulds. Finally, the chocolate is packed into boxes and delivered to shops. The process requires careful control of temperature at several stages, especially during roasting and heating.

*148 từ.*

#### Language bank — process

- `First, ...`
- `After that, ...`
- `Next, ...`
- `Once ... has/have ..., ...`
- `Finally, ...`
- Passive: `is/are + V3`
- Purpose: `to`, `in order to`

### 4.2 Map

#### Đề bài mẫu

> The maps show a coastal town in 2000 and 2025.

**Thay đổi (fictional):**

- 2000: farmland in the north, small harbour in the south, narrow road along the coast, park in the centre.
- 2025: farmland replaced by a hotel and shopping centre; harbour unchanged; new road built; park extended; car park added.

#### Model answer

> The maps compare the layout of a coastal town in 2000 and 2025. Overall, the town became more developed, with new tourist facilities and transport infrastructure.
>
> In 2000, the north of the town was mainly farmland, while the south had a small harbour. A narrow road ran along the coast, and there was a park in the centre. By 2025, the farmland had been replaced by a hotel and a shopping centre. The harbour remained unchanged, but a new road was built along the coast.
>
> The park in the centre was extended, and a car park was added next to it. The main road was also widened to handle more traffic. Overall, the town changed significantly, moving from a quiet fishing area to a more commercial tourist destination.

*140 từ.*

#### Language bank — map

- `in the north/south/east/west of`
- `to the north of`
- `next to`, `opposite`, `behind`, `along`
- `was replaced by`
- `was built/added/extended`
- `remained unchanged`
- `was converted into`

### 4.3 Guided practice

**1. Chọn bị động đúng.**

1. The beans **roast / are roasted** at a high temperature.
2. The mixture **pours / is poured** into moulds.
3. A new road **built / was built** in 2025.
4. The park **extended / was extended** last year.

**Đáp án:** 1. are roasted · 2. is poured · 3. was built · 4. was extended

**2. Viết câu từ gợi ý.**

- farmland / replace / hotel
- Gợi ý: The farmland was replaced by a hotel.

### 4.4 Write

Chọn **một**:

**A. Process:** The diagram shows how coffee is made. Steps: beans harvested → dried → roasted → ground → mixed with hot water → filtered → packed. Viết 150–170 từ.

**B. Map:** The maps show a university campus in 2010 and 2025. Changes: old library replaced by a science building; new dormitory built; sports field extended; car park added. Viết 150–170 từ.

### 4.5 Sample answer — Process

> The diagram shows how coffee is made, from harvesting the beans to packing the final product. Overall, there are seven main stages in the process.
>
> First, coffee beans are harvested by hand and collected in large baskets. They are then dried in the sun for several days. After that, the beans are roasted at a high temperature and ground into a fine powder. Next, the powder is mixed with hot water.
>
> The mixture is then filtered to remove the solid pieces. Finally, the coffee is packed into bags or jars and delivered to shops. The process is relatively simple, but it requires careful attention at the roasting and filtering stages, as these steps affect the flavour of the final product.

*126 từ.*

### 4.6 Sample answer — Map

> The maps compare a university campus in 2010 and 2025. Overall, the campus became larger and more modern, with new facilities for science and student housing.
>
> In 2010, the old library was in the centre of the campus, next to the main hall. The sports field was in the east, and there was a small car park near the entrance. By 2025, the old library had been replaced by a science building. A new dormitory was built to the north of the main hall, and the sports field was extended.
>
> The car park was also enlarged to provide more spaces. The main hall remained unchanged, but a new path was added between the science building and the dormitory. Overall, the campus changed significantly to meet the needs of a growing student population.

*140 từ.*

### 4.7 Common mistakes

| ❌ Sai | ✅ Đúng | Mã |
|---|---|---|
| The beans roast. | The beans **are roasted**. | VOICE |
| It was build in 2025. | It was **built** in 2025. | V3 |
| The park is in north of the town. | ... in **the** north of the town. | ART |
| The field replaced by a hotel. | The field **was** replaced by a hotel. | PASS |

## Unit 5 — Task 2 Structure + Opinion Essay

**Mục tiêu:** Viết Task 2 đúng cấu trúc; có thesis rõ; phát triển 2 lý do; kết bài không thêm ý mới.  
**Sản phẩm:** 250–270 từ.  
**Thời gian:** 40 phút.

### 5.1 Cấu trúc Task 2

1. **Introduction** — paraphrase đề + thesis.
2. **Body 1** — lý do 1 + giải thích + ví dụ.
3. **Body 2** — lý do 2 + giải thích + ví dụ.
4. **Conclusion** — tóm ý + nhắc quan điểm.

### 5.2 Đề bài mẫu

> Some people believe that university education should be free for everyone. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

### 5.3 Model answer

> It is often argued that university education should be provided free of charge for all students. While I agree that cost should not prevent people from studying, I believe that a completely free system is difficult to maintain and that some contribution from graduates is reasonable.
>
> The main reason for free university education is equality. Many talented students cannot afford tuition fees, especially those from low-income families. If university is free, these students can develop their skills and improve their future job prospects. For example, a student from a rural area may be unable to pay for both tuition and living costs, even though she has strong academic ability. Free education would give her a fair chance.
>
> However, free university education is expensive for the government. Taxpayers would have to cover the full cost, and there may be less money for other public services such as healthcare or primary education. In addition, university graduates usually earn higher salaries than people without degrees. It seems fair that they should contribute something after they start working. A system of income-based repayment could allow graduates to pay back part of the cost when they can afford it.
>
> In conclusion, I agree that financial barriers should be removed, but I do not think university should be completely free for everyone. A mixed system, with government support for low-income students and affordable contributions from graduates, would be both fair and sustainable.

*251 từ.*

### 5.4 Phân tích

- **Thesis rõ:** đồng ý một phần, không cực đoan.
- **Body 1:** equality + ví dụ cụ thể.
- **Body 2:** cost + fairness + giải pháp.
- **Conclusion:** tóm ý, không thêm lý do mới.

### 5.5 Language bank — opinion

- `I largely agree that ...`
- `While I agree that ..., I believe ...`
- `The main reason for this is ...`
- `For example, ...`
- `However, ...`
- `It seems fair that ...`
- `In conclusion, ...`

### 5.6 Guided practice

**1. Viết thesis cho đề:**

> Some people think that all students should study a foreign language at school. Do you agree?

**Gợi ý:** I agree that foreign languages should be taught at school, but I believe students should be able to choose which language to study.

**2. Chọn câu thesis tốt hơn.**

- A. This essay will discuss foreign languages.
- B. I believe foreign languages are valuable, but the choice of language should be flexible.

**Đáp án:** B.

**3. Sắp xếp body paragraph:**

> a. For example, ...  
> b. One reason is that ...  
> c. This means that ...  
> d. In conclusion, ...

**Đáp án:** b → c → a (d là conclusion, không nằm trong body).

### 5.7 Write

**Đề bài:** Some people think that young people should do unpaid work in their community. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Viết 250–270 từ.

**Checklist:**

- [ ] Intro paraphrase + thesis.
- [ ] Body 1 có topic sentence, giải thích, ví dụ.

### 5.8 Sample answer

> Some people argue that young people should spend time doing unpaid work in their community. I largely agree with this idea, although I believe the work should be carefully organised and should not replace paid employment.
>
> One benefit is that unpaid community work helps young people develop responsibility. When they help at a local centre, clean a park or support older residents, they learn that their actions affect other people. For example, a student who volunteers at a food bank may understand the problem of poverty more clearly than someone who only reads about it. This experience can also improve their confidence and teamwork skills.
>
> Another advantage is that community work can improve social connections. Young people often spend most of their time with people of the same age. Volunteering brings them into contact with children, older people and people from different backgrounds. This can reduce loneliness in the community and help young people understand different lives.
>
> However, unpaid work should not become a burden. Some young people already have heavy study schedules or part-time jobs. If community service is required, schools should provide flexible options and clear support. It should also not be used to replace paid workers.
>
> In conclusion, I agree that unpaid community work is valuable for young people, but it should be optional or carefully balanced with study and paid work.

## Unit 6 — Discussion Essay

**Mục tiêu:** Trình bày hai quan điểm cân bằng; nêu quan điểm cá nhân; dùng concession.  
**Sản phẩm:** 250–270 từ.  
**Thời gian:** 40 phút.

### 6.1 Đề bài mẫu

> Some people prefer to work from home, while others prefer to work in an office. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

### 6.2 Model answer

> The way people work has changed significantly in recent years. While some employees enjoy the flexibility of working from home, others prefer the structure and social contact of an office. In my view, a hybrid arrangement is often the best solution.
>
> Those who support working from home point to flexibility and saved time. Employees do not need to commute, so they can start work earlier and spend more time with their families. They can also organise their day around tasks that require concentration. For example, a writer or programmer may work more effectively in a quiet home office than in a busy open-plan office. In addition, companies can save money on office space.
>
> On the other hand, office work offers clear benefits. Communication is often faster when colleagues can speak face to face. New employees can learn from experienced staff by watching and asking questions. The office also provides social contact, which can reduce loneliness. For instance, a short conversation in the kitchen may solve a problem that would take several emails to explain. Some people also find it easier to separate work from home life when they leave the office.
>
> In my opinion, neither option is perfect for everyone. A hybrid model allows employees to focus at home on some days and collaborate in the office on others. However, it requires good planning and clear communication.
>
> In conclusion, both remote work and office work have advantages. I believe that a flexible combination is more practical than choosing one extreme.

*251 từ.*

> Education at home and education at school are both common in different parts of the world. While home schooling offers flexibility, I believe that school is usually better for most children because it provides social development and structured learning.

For example, a child who is very good at mathematics can move ahead quickly, while a child who struggles with reading can spend more time on it. > In conclusion, although home schooling has advantages, I believe that school is generally the better option for children’s academic and social development.

## Unit 7 — Problem–Solution Essay

**Mục tiêu:** Nêu 2 vấn đề và 2 giải pháp; giải thích mối liên hệ; dùng ngôn ngữ nguyên nhân–kết quả.  
**Sản phẩm:** 250–270 từ.  
**Thời gian:** 40 phút.

### 7.1 Đề bài mẫu

> Traffic congestion is a serious problem in many cities. What problems does it cause, and what solutions can you suggest?

### 7.2 Model answer

> Traffic congestion has become a common problem in large cities, especially during rush hour. It causes serious environmental and economic problems, but there are several practical solutions.
>
> One major problem is air pollution. When cars move slowly or stop in traffic, they burn more fuel and produce more exhaust fumes. This can lead to breathing problems and contribute to climate change. Congestion also wastes time. Workers may arrive late, and businesses lose money because deliveries are delayed. In addition, drivers often feel stressed and frustrated, which can affect their health and behaviour.
>
> One solution is to improve public transport. If buses and trains are frequent, reliable and affordable, more people will leave their cars at home. For example, a city with a fast metro system may reduce the number of cars in the centre. Another solution is congestion charging. Drivers who enter the busiest areas at peak times pay a fee, and the money can be used to improve public transport. This has worked in some cities, although it must be designed fairly for low-income residents.
>
> Finally, city planners should make walking and cycling safer. Good pavements, bike lanes and crossings can encourage people to use these forms of transport for short journeys.
>
> In conclusion, traffic congestion causes pollution, delays and stress. The most effective response is a combination of better public transport, fair charges and safer routes for walking and cycling.

*247 từ.*

### 7.6 Sample answer

> Plastic waste has become a serious environmental problem in many countries. It harms wildlife, pollutes the oceans and takes hundreds of years to break down. However, several solutions can reduce the damage.
>
> One problem is that plastic often ends up in rivers and oceans. Animals may mistake it for food, and they can become sick or die. Plastic also breaks into tiny pieces called microplastics, which enter the food chain. In addition, burning plastic can release harmful chemicals into the air, affecting human health.
>
> One solution is to reduce the use of single-use plastic. Governments can ban plastic bags and encourage shops to use paper or reusable bags. For example, some cities charge a small fee for plastic bags, which encourages customers to bring their own. Another solution is to improve recycling systems. >
> Finally, companies should design products that are easier to reuse or repair. This would reduce the amount of plastic that is thrown away.
>
> In conclusion, plastic waste causes serious harm to animals, oceans and human health. The best response is to reduce single-use plastic, improve recycling and encourage companies to design more sustainable products.

#### Sample answer

> It is often argued that governments should invest more in public transport than in new roads. I largely agree with this view, although I believe that road improvements are still necessary in some areas.
>
> The main reason for prioritising public transport is that it can reduce congestion and pollution. A single bus can carry many more people than a car, so it uses less road space per passenger. If buses and trains are frequent and reliable, more people will leave their cars at home. >

The solution is not to stop building roads, but to make sure that public transport receives a larger share of investment.
>
> In conclusion, I agree that governments should prioritise public transport because it reduces congestion, pollution and inequality. At the same time, road spending should continue where it is truly necessary.
