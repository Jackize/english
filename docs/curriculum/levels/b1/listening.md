# B1 Listening — IELTS Academic Foundation

**Level:** B1 (CEFR) · **IELTS:** khoảng 4.5–5.5 · **Số unit:** 9 (8 bài học + 1 mock test)  
**Kỹ năng:** form completion, map/directions, MCQ, matching, notes, table completion, signposting  
**Ngôn ngữ:** hướng dẫn tiếng Việt; script, câu hỏi và đáp án tiếng Anh

> Tài liệu luyện **IELTS Academic Listening** ở mức B1, không phải đề thi thật. Mục tiêu: nghe hiểu đoạn 2–5 phút, làm đúng phần lớn câu hỏi và tự sửa lỗi bằng transcript.

---

## 0. Giới thiệu & cách dùng

### 0.1 Sau 9 unit, bạn có thể

- Nghe hội thoại/bài giảng 2–5 phút ở 120–140 từ/phút, có distractor.
- Điền form, notes, table đúng giới hạn từ; viết đúng số, ngày, giá, tên.
- Nhận ra signposting: *first, in contrast, another example, however, to sum up*.

### 0.2 Dùng audio nguồn ngoài hợp pháp

Mỗi bài học (Unit 1–8) có 2 link đến **trang gốc**, không phải audio sao chép.

- Nghe trực tiếp trên trang; không tải rồi đăng lại.
- Không vượt paywall; chỉ dùng link trang chủ/series và ghi tên nguồn khi chia sẻ.
- Không dùng tên/logo của British Council, BBC, IELTS hay Cambridge gây hiểu nhầm đây là sản phẩm chính thức.
- Script trong tài liệu là **script gốc** viết cho B1; được dùng để học, in cho lớp hoặc tạo audio TTS cá nhân.

Không có audio? Dùng TTS 0.9–1.0x hoặc nhờ người đọc; không nhìn script ở lần 1–2.

### 0.3 Phương pháp nghe 3 lần

| Lần | Mục đích | Cách làm | Transcript? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Gist | Ai? Ở đâu? Chuyện gì? | Nghe trọn bài; trả lời 3 câu gist | Không |
| 2 — Detail | Số, ngày, tên, lý do, địa điểm | Nghe 2 lần, dừng sau 2–3 câu; làm detail task | Không |
| 3 — Dictation | Âm, từ, trọng âm, nối âm | Dừng từng câu, viết lại, so với script | Có, sau khi viết |

**Lần 2:** đánh dấu *actually, sorry, I mean, no wait* — thông tin **sau** cụm sửa thường là đáp án.  
**Lần 3:** viết đủ câu, kể cả *a, the, at, on*; khoanh lỗi cuối từ, số nhiều, thì quá khứ, giới từ; đọc đuổi theo audio.

**Sau 48 giờ:** nghe lại không nhìn script; hiểu và làm đúng ≥80% là đạt.

### 0.4 Cách chấm điểm

Mỗi bài học có **13 hoặc 14 điểm**: Gist 3, Detail 5–6, Dictation 5. Bài 2 có 13 điểm; các bài còn lại 14 điểm. Unit 9 có 20 điểm.

| Tỷ lệ đúng | Đánh giá | Việc tiếp theo |
|---:|---|---|
| 90–100% | Rất tốt | Học bài tiếp; ôn sau 3 ngày |
| 70–89% | Đạt B1 | Sửa câu sai; học bài tiếp |
| 50–69% | Cần ôn | Nghe lại chậm 0.85x sau 2 ngày |
| dưới 50% | Chưa đạt | Đọc script, nghe lại 3 lần, làm lại |

**Dictation:** 1 điểm/câu. Bỏ qua dấu câu và viết hoa. Sai một từ làm đổi nghĩa → 0 điểm; sai chính tả nhẹ không đổi nghĩa → 0.5 điểm. Với form/notes/table, thiếu *-s* hoặc sai tên riêng được tính là sai.

> Điểm chỉ để theo dõi tiến bộ, không quy đổi band IELTS.

### 0.5 Lỗi thường gặp của người Việt

| Lỗi | Ví dụ | Cách sửa |
|---|---|---|
| Bỏ âm cuối | *worked* → /wɜːk/ | Đọc rõ /kt/, /pt/, /st/; luyện đuôi -ed |
| Bỏ -s | *books* → /bʊk/ | Nghe đuôi /s/, /z/, /ɪz/ |
| /ɪ/ và /iː/ | *sit* — *seat* | Kéo dài /iː/; nghe cặp tối thiểu |
| /æ/ và /e/ | *bad* — *bed* | Mở miệng rộng hơn với /æ/ |
| /θ/, /ð/ | *three* — *tree* | Đặt lưỡi giữa răng |
| Số -teen/-ty | *fifteen* — *fifty* | Nghe trọng âm: -teen nhấn cuối |
| Distractor | chọn thông tin đầu tiên | Chờ *actually, sorry, I mean* |

## Unit 1 — Section 1: Form completion (accommodation)

**Mục tiêu:** Nghe cuộc gọi đặt phòng; điền tên, ngày, loại phòng, thời hạn, giá, số điện thoại; nhận ra thông tin bị sửa.  
**Script:** 300 từ · 2 người nói.

### 1.1 Vocabulary & pronunciation

| Word/phrase | IPA | Nghĩa |
|---|---|---|
| en-suite | /ˌɒn ˈswiːt/ | có phòng tắm riêng |
| studio | /ˈstjuːdiəʊ/ | căn hộ một phòng |
| contract | /ˈkɒntrækt/ | hợp đồng |
| deposit | /dɪˈpɒzɪt/ | tiền đặt cọc |

### 1.2 External listening

- **British Council B1:** https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/skills/listening/b1-listening — bài *A phone call from a customer*.
- **ESL Lab:** https://www.esl-lab.com/ — mục *Intermediate*, bài booking/hotel.

### 1.3 Script

> **Receptionist:** Good afternoon, Harbour Student Residence. How can I help?
>
> **Mai:** Hello. I’d like to book a room for the new term. I saw the en-suite single on your website.
>
> **Receptionist:** Yes, we have two left. It costs seven hundred and twenty pounds a month, including bills.
>
> **Mai:** That’s a little over my budget. Is there anything cheaper?
>
> **Receptionist:** We have a studio for six hundred and ninety pounds a month. It has a small kitchen and its own bathroom, and bills are included.
>
> **Mai:** That sounds better. Is it on the ground floor?
>
> **Receptionist:** It’s on the second floor, but there is a lift. We provide a bed, a desk and a wardrobe, but not bedding.
>
> **Mai:** That’s fine. I’ll take the studio.
>
> **Receptionist:** When would you like to move in?
>
> **Mai:** My course starts on the fifteenth of September. Could I move in on the twelfth?
>
> **Receptionist:** The twelfth is a Saturday, and we don’t do weekend check-ins. The first available day is Monday the fourteenth.
>
> **Mai:** The fourteenth is fine.
>
> **Receptionist:** And how long do you need the room for?
>
> **Mai:** About ten months. I might go home in June.
>
> **Receptionist:** The shortest contract we offer is forty weeks. After that, you can extend it if the room is free.
>
> **Mai:** Forty weeks works for me.
>
> **Receptionist:** Can I confirm your full name?
>
> **Mai:** Mai Nguyen. That’s N-G-U-Y-E-N.
>
> **Receptionist:** Thank you. And a mobile number?
>
> **Mai:** It’s zero seven seven three four, two one eight, nine six zero. Sorry, the last three digits are nine six zero, not nine zero six.
>
> **Receptionist:** So that’s zero seven seven three four, two one eight, nine six zero?
>
> **Mai:** Yes, that’s right.
>
> **Receptionist:** We’ll need a deposit of three hundred pounds, but you can pay that when you arrive.
>
> **Mai:** Is the deposit refundable?
>
> **Receptionist:** Yes, as long as the room is clean and nothing is damaged.
>
> **Mai:** Great. Thank you very much. That’s very helpful.

### 1.4 Gist — 3 câu

Chọn **A**, **B** hoặc **C**.

1. Mai is calling to **A** ask about a course **B** book accommodation **C** complain.
2. Mai refuses the first room because it is **A** small **B** expensive **C** far away.
3. At the end she gives **A** a card payment **B** contact details **C** an email.

### 1.5 Detail — Form completion (6 câu)

Viết **NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER** cho mỗi câu.

| Harbour Student Residence — Booking Form | |
|---|---|
| Name | **4** ______ |
| Move-in date | **5** ______ |
| Room type | **6** ______ |
| Length of contract | **7** ______ |
| Monthly rent | £ **8** ______ |
| Mobile number | **9** ______ |

### 1.6 Dictation — 5 câu

10. The studio costs six hundred and ninety pounds a month.
11. The first available day is Monday the fourteenth of September.
12. I’m sorry, the last three digits are nine six zero.
13. The contract lasts for forty weeks.
14. The rent includes all bills.

### 1.7 Answer key

**Gist:** 1 **B** · 2 **B** · 3 **B**  
**Detail:** 4 **Mai Nguyen** · 5 **14 September** · 6 **studio** · 7 **40 weeks** · 8 **690** · 9 **07734 218 960**  
**Dictation:** 10–14 — đúng nguyên văn các câu in ở mục 1.6.

### 1.8 Study method

Nghe lại đoạn giá và số điện thoại; đọc to *fourteen/forty*; shadowing 30 giây. Sau 48 giờ làm lại form không xem script.

## Unit 2 — Section 1: Map labelling & directions

**Mục tiêu:** Theo dõi map campus; hiểu *north-west, next to, between, turn left/right*; trả lời 5 câu vị trí/hướng đi.  
**Script:** 300 từ · 1 người nói.

### 2.1 Vocabulary & pronunciation

| Word/phrase | IPA | Nghĩa |
|---|---|---|
| north-west | /ˌnɔːθ ˈwest/ | tây bắc |
| south-east | /ˌsaʊθ ˈiːst/ | đông nam |
| fountain | /ˈfaʊntən/ | đài phun nước |
| between | /bɪˈtwiːn/ | ở giữa |

### 2.2 The map

North is at the top. **College Road** runs east–west. **Park Lane** runs north–south.

| | West | Centre | East |
|---|---|---|---|
| **North** | Library | Car Park | Sports Centre |
| **Middle** | Main Gate | Fountain | Bus Stop |
| **South** | Student Union | Café | Medical Centre |

### 2.3 External listening

- **British Council B1:** https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/skills/listening/b1-listening — mục B1 listening; luyện thêm hội thoại đời sống.
- **ELLLO:** https://www.elllo.org/ — mục *directions, campus*.

### 2.4 Script

> **Guide:** Good morning, everyone, and welcome to Northfield Campus. I’m Jamie, and I’ll be your guide this morning. Before we walk around, let me explain the layout so you don’t get lost.
>
> You’re standing at the Main Gate, at the western end of College Road. If you look straight ahead, you’ll see the fountain in the centre of the campus.
>
> The library is in the north-west corner, next to the car park. Some of you may have read that it is in the old science building. That was true until last year, but the library moved into the new building in August.
>
> The car park is directly north of the fountain. Beyond the car park, in the north-east corner, is the Sports Centre. It is next to the car park, not beside the library, so don’t turn left too early.
>
> Now, south of College Road. The Student Union is south-west of the fountain. From the Main Gate, walk to the fountain, turn right, and it is the first large building on your right. The café used to be inside the Student Union, but it now has its own building on the south side of College Road, between the Student Union and the Medical Centre. The Medical Centre is in the south-east corner.
>
> The bus stop is east of the fountain, just outside the east gate. A common mistake is to turn left at the fountain for the Medical Centre. Left takes you north, towards the car park and library. For the Medical Centre, you need to turn right and walk south.
>
> The tour is free today for all new students. We’ll start at the library. The tour lasts about forty minutes, so keep your bags with you and don’t cross the road without me. Now, any questions? Thank you. Let’s go.

### 2.5 Gist — 3 câu

Chọn **A**, **B** hoặc **C**.

1. Who speaks? **A** a student **B** a guide **C** a driver
2. Where are the listeners at the beginning? **A** Main Gate **B** library **C** bus stop
3. What has changed recently? **A** the library moved **B** the café closed **C** the Sports Centre expanded.

### 2.6 Detail — Map questions (5 câu)

Trả lời bằng **ONE WORD OR A SHORT PHRASE**.

4. Which building is in the north-west corner?
5. What is directly north of the fountain?
6. Which place is east of the fountain, outside the east gate?
7. Which building is between the Student Union and the Medical Centre?
8. At the fountain, which direction do you turn to reach the Medical Centre?

### 2.7 Dictation — 5 câu

9. The library is in the north-west corner, next to the car park.
10. Walk to the fountain and turn right.
11. The café is between the Student Union and the Medical Centre.
12. The Sports Centre is in the north-east corner of the campus.
13. Don’t turn left at the fountain if you want the Medical Centre.

### 2.8 Answer key

**Gist:** 1 **B** · 2 **A** · 3 **A**  
**Detail:** 4 **Library** · 5 **Car Park** · 6 **Bus Stop** · 7 **Café** · 8 **Right (then south)**  
**Dictation:** 9–13 — đúng nguyên văn các câu in ở mục 2.7.

### 2.9 Study method

Che map, vẽ lại 9 địa điểm; di ngón tay theo route; tự chỉ đường từ cổng đến thư viện trong 30 giây.

## Unit 3 — Section 2: Multiple choice (community event)

**Mục tiêu:** Nghe thông báo sự kiện; chọn đáp án đúng khi có thông tin nhiễu về ngày, địa điểm, giá và thời hạn.  
**Script:** 303 từ · 1 người nói.

### 3.1 Vocabulary & pronunciation

| Word/phrase | IPA | Nghĩa |
|---|---|---|
| repair | /rɪˈpeə/ | sửa chữa |
| volunteer | /ˌvɒlənˈtɪə/ | tình nguyện viên |
| donation | /dəʊˈneɪʃn/ | tiền quyên góp |
| spare part | /speə pɑːt/ | phụ tùng thay thế |

### 3.2 External listening

- **BBC 6 Minute English:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish/english/features/6-minute-english — tập về community/volunteering.
- **Breaking News English:** https://breakingnewsenglish.com/ — bài level 3–4 về community.

### 3.3 Script

> **Presenter:** Hello, and welcome to Community Radio. I’m Priya, one of the organisers of the Harbourside Repair Café. I’m here to tell you about our next event.
>
> We meet on the second Saturday of every month, not the first Saturday, as some of our old posters say. So this month, that’s the eighth of March, from ten in the morning until two in the afternoon.
>
> We used to meet at the library, but we’ve moved to the old town hall on Mill Street because we needed more space. The entrance is at the back of the building, next to the car park.
>
> What can we repair? We can help with clothes, bicycles, small electrical items and wooden furniture. We can’t repair mobile phones or laptops, I’m afraid. Those need a specialist. We also can’t accept dangerous items, such as microwaves.
>
> The event is free, but we welcome donations. Three pounds is the suggested amount, but you can give more or less. All the money goes towards tools and materials.
>
> If you have something to repair, please bring it with you. If possible, also bring a spare part, such as a plug or a bicycle inner tube. We have some parts, but we don’t have everything, and we can’t order parts on the day.
>
> Our volunteers are not all experts. Some are retired engineers, but others are learning. We run a short training session at half past nine for anyone who would like to help. You don’t need to book a place — just come along.
>
> One more thing: the last item is accepted at half past one, so please arrive early. The café next door sells tea and cake, and you’re welcome to wait there. Last year we repaired over three hundred items, and we hope to do even better this month. Thanks for listening!

### 3.4 Gist — 3 câu

Chọn **A**, **B** hoặc **C**.

1. What is Priya’s main purpose? **A** advertise an event **B** recruit staff **C** complain.
2. Priya is **A** a journalist **B** an organiser **C** a shop owner.
3. What kind of event is it? **A** a sale **B** a repair service **C** a cooking class.

### 3.5 Detail — Multiple choice (6 câu)

Chọn **A**, **B** hoặc **C**.

4. When is it held? **A** first Saturday **B** second Saturday **C** every Sunday
5. Where is it held now? **A** the library **B** the old town hall **C** the community centre
6. What can they repair? **A** phones **B** laptops **C** bicycles
7. What should people bring? **A** a spare part **B** a ticket **C** a form
8. How much does it cost? **A** free, donations welcome **B** £3 exactly **C** £5 per item
9. When is the last item accepted? **A** 1:30 **B** 2:00 **C** 10:00

### 3.6 Dictation — 5 câu

10. The Repair Café is held on the second Saturday of every month.
11. We have moved to the old town hall on Mill Street.
12. We can repair clothes, bicycles and small electrical items.
13. Please bring the broken item and a spare part if possible.
14. The last item is accepted at half past one.

### 3.7 Answer key

**Gist:** 1 **A** · 2 **B** · 3 **B**  
**Detail:** 4 **B** · 5 **B** · 6 **C** · 7 **A** · 8 **A** · 9 **A**  
**Dictation:** 10–14 — đúng nguyên văn các câu in ở mục 3.6.

### 3.8 Study method

Gạch *when/where/how much*; đánh dấu *not/but/now*; shadowing 30 giây.

## Unit 4 — Section 2: Matching (facilities and services)

**Mục tiêu:** Nghe mô tả phòng/chức năng; nối đúng facility; phân biệt mục đích, điều kiện và giới hạn sử dụng.  
**Script:** 305 từ · 1 người nói.

### 4.1 Vocabulary & pronunciation

| Word/phrase | IPA | Nghĩa |
|---|---|---|
| facility | /fəˈsɪləti/ | cơ sở vật chất |
| supervisor | /ˈsuːpəvaɪzə/ | người giám sát |
| workshop | /ˈwɜːkʃɒp/ | xưởng |
| safety course | /ˈseɪfti kɔːs/ | khóa an toàn |

### 4.2 External listening

- **British Council B1:** https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/skills/listening/b1-listening — mục B1 listening; luyện thêm hội thoại đời sống.
- **ESL Lab:** https://www.esl-lab.com/ — mục *Intermediate*, bài community centre.

### 4.3 Script

> **Manager:** Hello, and welcome to Greenfield Community Centre. My name is Sarah, and I manage the building. I’m going to describe our main rooms, because they are used for different activities.
>
> The Main Hall is our largest space. It can seat two hundred people, not one hundred and fifty as the old website says. We use it for concerts, plays and large meetings. It has a stage, but no cooking facilities. There is also a hearing loop for people who need it.
>
> Next to it is the Kitchen. Community groups can use it for cooking classes, but a trained supervisor must be present. You can’t hire the kitchen for a private party, I’m afraid. The supervisor is included in the room price.
>
> The Study Room is on the first floor. It has ten computers and a printer. It’s busy during the day, but it’s usually quiet in the evening. Students can book it for two hours at a time. It is not a library, so please don’t bring your own food in.
>
> Downstairs, the Garden Room has doors that open onto a small outdoor area. It’s popular for children’s birthday parties because the children can play outside. There are tables and chairs, but no cooking facilities.
>
> The Sports Hall is at the end of the corridor. You can play badminton, basketball and indoor football there. We don’t have a swimming pool, so please don’t ask me about swimming lessons!
>
> Finally, the Workshop at the back of the building has tools for woodwork and repairs. It’s only open to members who have completed a safety course. We run the course on the first Monday of each month. Without the course, you can’t use the equipment, even if you are experienced.
>
> If you’d like to book a room, please speak to me or send an email. Thank you.

### 4.4 Gist — 3 câu

Chọn **A**, **B** hoặc **C**.

1. Who is the speaker? **A** a manager **B** a student **C** a builder
2. Where is she speaking? **A** a sports club **B** a community centre **C** a hotel
3. What is the purpose of the talk? **A** describe rooms **B** sell equipment **C** recruit staff.

### 4.5 Detail — Matching (6 câu)

Nối mỗi mô tả với cơ sở vật chất, **A–F**. Mỗi lựa chọn dùng **một lần**.

**A** Main Hall · **B** Kitchen · **C** Study Room · **D** Garden Room · **E** Sports Hall · **F** Workshop

4. Good for a children’s party; has an outdoor area. ______
5. Used for badminton and basketball. ______
6. Has computers; quiet in the evening. ______
7. Used for cooking classes with a supervisor. ______
8. Seats 200 people; has a stage. ______
9. Has tools; a safety course is required. ______

### 4.6 Dictation — 5 câu

10. The Main Hall can seat two hundred people.
11. The Garden Room has a small outdoor area.
12. The kitchen can only be used with a trained supervisor.
13. You need to complete a safety course before using the workshop.
14. The study room is quiet in the evening.

### 4.7 Answer key

**Gist:** 1 **A** · 2 **B** · 3 **A**  
**Detail:** 4 **D** · 5 **E** · 6 **C** · 7 **B** · 8 **A** · 9 **F**  
**Dictation:** 10–14 — đúng nguyên văn các câu in ở mục 4.6.

### 4.8 Study method

Đọc options, gạch keyword; viết chữ cái khi nghe; kiểm tra mỗi option dùng một lần.

## Unit 5 — Section 3: Discussion and notes (student project)

**Mục tiêu:** Nghe hai sinh viên bàn dự án; điền notes về mục tiêu, phương pháp, phỏng vấn, ngày thuyết trình và phần kết.  
**Script:** 300 từ · 2 người nói.

### 5.1 Vocabulary & pronunciation

| Word/phrase | IPA | Nghĩa |
|---|---|---|
| food waste | /fuːd weɪst/ | rác thải thực phẩm |
| weigh | /weɪ/ | cân |
| survey | /ˈsɜːveɪ/ | khảo sát |
| recommendation | /ˌrekəmenˈdeɪʃn/ | khuyến nghị |

### 5.2 External listening

- **British Council B1:** https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/skills/listening/b1-listening — bài *A student discussion*.
- **ELLLO:** https://www.elllo.org/ — mục *university project, group work*.

### 5.3 Script

> **Leo:** Hi, An. Have you started planning our food waste project?
>
> **An:** I’ve made a few notes, but I’m not sure where to begin. We need to measure how much food the student café throws away.
>

### 7.3 Script

> **Lecturer:** Good morning. Today’s lecture is about sleep, and why it matters for students. I’m going to describe what happens when we sleep, and then give you a few practical suggestions.
>
> First, sleep is not a passive state. The brain is very active. During sleep, it sorts information from the day and stores important memories. This is one reason why sleeping after studying can help you remember more.
>
> Sleep is made up of different stages. Deep sleep is important for the body: it helps us to repair muscles and tissue. REM sleep, which is when we dream, is especially important for learning and creativity. You need both kinds, not just one.
>
> How much sleep do adults need? Most adults need between seven and nine hours a night. Eight is a common average, but the right amount varies from person to person. Teenagers usually need more. Students often sleep less during exam periods, and that can affect concentration and mood.
>
> Two systems control sleep. One is sleep pressure, which builds up while you are awake. The other is your body clock, which follows light and darkness. Light in the morning helps reset the body clock, so try to go outside soon after you wake up. In the evening, bright screens can make it harder to fall asleep.
>
> What about naps? A short nap of about twenty minutes can improve alertness. However, a long nap in the afternoon can make it harder to fall asleep at night. If you drink coffee, remember that caffeine stays in the body for several hours. Try not to drink it after early afternoon.
>
> Good. So sleep also affects mood and decision-making, so it is not a luxury. To sum up: keep a regular routine, get daylight in the morning, and protect your sleep before exams. Thank you.

## Unit 8 — Section 4: Table completion and signposting (biomimicry)

**Mục tiêu:** Theo dõi signposting; điền table về phát minh lấy cảm hứng từ tự nhiên.  
**Script:** 304 từ · 1 người nói.

| drag | /dræɡ/ | lực cản |

### 8.2 External listening

- **BBC 6 Minute English:** https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish/english/features/6-minute-english — tập về inventions/nature.
- **Breaking News English:** https://breakingnewsenglish.com/ — bài level 4 về science.

### 8.3 Script

> **Lecturer:** Today I’m going to talk about biomimicry — using ideas from nature to solve engineering problems. It is a young science, but it is growing quickly. The word comes from the Greek words for life and imitation. Nature has had millions of years to test solutions, so it is a good place to look for ideas.
>
> First, let’s look at transport. In Japan, engineers redesigned the nose of a bullet train after studying the kingfisher. The kingfisher is a small bird that dives into water with very little splash. The new train shape reduced noise and used less energy. Some people think the design came from a shark, but that’s not correct.
>
> In contrast, the lotus leaf has inspired a completely different technology. Lotus leaves stay clean because water rolls off them and takes dirt with it. Scientists copied this effect to make paint that needs less water for cleaning. Another example is the termite mound. Its shape keeps the inside cool, so architects designed a building with a cooling system that uses less energy.
>
> Next, shark skin. It has tiny scales that reduce drag in water. Swimsuit designers used a similar pattern to help swimmers move faster. And Velcro came from burrs — the small hooks that stick to clothes. The inventor noticed them after a walk, and he copied the way they fasten.
>
> Finally, spider silk is stronger than steel for its weight. Researchers are trying to produce a strong, light rope based on it, although making it in large quantities is still difficult.
>
> Of course, copying nature is not always easy. Natural systems are complex, and a design that works in a forest may not work in a city. Every idea needs testing.
>
> To sum up, biomimicry is not about copying how nature looks. It is about copying how nature works. Thank you.
