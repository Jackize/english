# Placement Test — Xếp level A1 → C2

> Bài test nhanh 25–30 phút để chọn điểm bắt đầu. Không phải bài thi IELTS chính thức. Kết quả chỉ mang tính gợi ý.

---

## 1. Cách làm

1. Làm phần Grammar + Vocabulary + Reading tanpa tra từ.
2. Viết 1 đoạn 80–120 từ (Writing).
3. Ghi âm 1–2 phút trả lời 3 câu Speaking.
4. Chấm theo bảng ở cuối.
5. Nếu kết quả nằm giữa hai level, chọn level thấp hơn để xây nền.

---

## 2. Grammar & Vocabulary (20 câu)

Chọn đáp án đúng.

### Grammar

1. She ___ a teacher.
   - A. am
   - B. is
   - C. are
   - D. be

2. I ___ to school every day.
   - A. go
   - B. goes
   - C. going
   - D. went

3. There ___ two books on the table.
   - A. is
   - B. are
   - C. be
   - D. was

4. He ___ TV when I called him.
   - A. watches
   - B. watched
   - C. was watching
   - D. watch

5. This book is ___ than that one.
   - A. interesting
   - B. more interesting
   - C. most interesting
   - D. interessant

6. I have lived here ___ 2020.
   - A. for
   - B. since
   - C. from
   - D. in

7. If it ___ tomorrow, we will stay at home.
   - A. rains
   - B. will rain
   - C. rained
   - D. raining

8. She asked me ___ I had finished the report.
   - A. that
   - B. if
   - C. what
   - D. which

9. The report ___ by the manager yesterday.
   - A. wrote
   - B. was written
   - C. has written
   - D. is writing

10. Hardly ___ the meeting when the lights went out.
    - A. we had started
    - B. had we started
    - C. we started
    - D. did we start

## 3. Reading (5 câu)

Read the passage and choose the best answer.

> Many cities are trying to reduce the number of cars in their centres. Some have introduced congestion charges, while others have built better public transport systems. Supporters say these measures cut pollution and make streets safer. Critics argue that they can be unfair to people who cannot afford to live near public transport. A successful policy, therefore, needs to offer practical alternatives before it restricts car use.

1. What are many cities trying to do?
   - A. Build more roads
   - B. Reduce cars in city centres
   - C. Increase parking spaces
   - D. Ban public transport

2. Supporters believe congestion charges can ...
   - A. increase pollution
   - B. make streets safer
   - C. help car companies
   - D. reduce public transport

3. Critics say the measures can be unfair to ...
   - A. city planners
   - B. people without good public transport
   - C. car manufacturers
   - D. tourists

4. The word “restricts” is closest in meaning to ...
   - A. limits
   - B. encourages
   - C. explains
   - D. improves

5. What does the passage suggest?
   - A. Cities should ban all cars immediately.
   - B. Public transport is not important.
   - C. Policies need practical alternatives.
   - D. Congestion charges always fail.

## 4. Writing (80–120 từ)

Chọn 1 đề:

1. **A1–A2:** Describe your daily routine. What do you do in the morning, afternoon and evening?
2. **A2–B1:** Some people prefer to work from home; others prefer to work in an office. What are the advantages and disadvantages?
3. **B1–B2:** The chart below shows the percentage of people using different types of transport in a city between 2000 and 2020. Summarise the information and make comparisons.
4. **B2–C2:** “Governments should invest more in public transport than in new roads.” To what extent do you agree or disagree?

> Nếu chọn đề 3, tự vẽ hoặc tưởng tượng số liệu đơn giản: car 60% → 35%, bus 20% → 30%, bike 10% → 25%, walking 10% → 10%.

## 5. Speaking (ghi âm 1–2 phút)

Trả lời 3 câu:

1. Tell me about your hometown.
2. What do you usually do in your free time?
3. Do you think people will read fewer books in the future? Why?

## 6. Answer key

### Grammar

1. B — `She is`
2. A — present simple với `I`
3. B — `two books` số nhiều
4. C — past continuous
5. B — so sánh hơn với tính từ dài
6. B — `since` + mốc thời gian
7. A — first conditional: if + present simple
8. B — reported question với `if`
9. B — passive past simple
10. B — đảo ngữ với `hardly`

### Vocabulary

11. A — `book a hotel`
12. A — `a headache`
13. A — `launch a product`
14. A — `reduce the amount`
15. A — `closely related`
16. B — `convincing argument`
17. A — `policy`
18. A — `The report suggests ...` (chủ ngữ số ít)
19. B — `great passion`
20. A — `come into effect`

### Reading

1. B
2. B
3. B
4. A
5. C

## 8. Kết quả gợi ý

- **A1:** bắt đầu A1 pack, 20–30 phút/ngày.
- **A2:** bắt đầu A2 pack, có thể ôn nhanh A1 nếu Writing/Speaking yếu.
- **B1:** bắt đầu B1 pack; làm placement Reading/Listening đầy đủ trước.
- **B2:** B2 pack; tập trung Task 1/2 và Speaking Part 3.
- **C1–C2:** C1/C2 pack; ưu tiên full mock, error audit, academic vocabulary.

## 9. Lưu ý

- Bài test này không thay thế bài thi IELTS.
- Không nên chọn level chỉ vì điểm Grammar; Writing/Speaking/Listening cũng quan trọng.
- Nếu kết quả giữa hai level, chọn level thấp hơn 2–4 tuần rồi kiểm tra lại.
