# B1 Speaking — IELTS Academic Foundation

**Level:** B1 (CEFR) · **IELTS:** khoảng 4.5–5.5 · **Số topic:** 12  
**Kỹ năng:** IELTS Speaking Part 1–3  
**Ngôn ngữ:** hướng dẫn tiếng Việt; câu hỏi, cụm từ và bài mẫu tiếng Anh

> Đây là level đầu tiên luyện **full IELTS Speaking**. Mục tiêu là trả lời rõ ràng, có lý do và ví dụ, duy trì 4–5 phút, không học thuộc lòng. Bạn không cần nói như người bản xứ; cần nói **mạch lạc, dễ hiểu, có kiểm soát**.

---

## 0. Giới thiệu & cách học

### 0.1 Sau 12 topic, bạn có thể

- Trả lời Part 1 bằng 2–4 câu, tự nhiên, có chi tiết.
- Nói Part 2 trong 1,5–2 phút theo cue card, có mở–thân–kết.
- Trả lời Part 3 bằng quan điểm + lý do + ví dụ + concession đơn giản.
- Dùng 150–200 cụm từ chủ đề và 10 khung câu lập luận.
- Tự ghi âm, nghe lại, sửa 2–3 lỗi mỗi lần.
- Tự chấm bằng rubric 1–5 và biết mình cần luyện gì.

### 0.2 Cách luyện một topic

1. **Đọc câu hỏi, trả lời miệng 1 lần** (không chuẩn bị).
2. **Học 10–15 cụm từ**, đọc to.
3. **Dùng outline**, nói lại 1–2 phút.
4. **Ghi âm**, nghe lại, ghi 2 lỗi.
5. **So với model**, lấy 1–2 cụm hay.
6. **Ghi âm lại**, cố gắng dùng cụm mới.
7. **Ôn lại sau 2 ngày.**

### 0.3 Không học thuộc lòng

Model answer là **nguồn ngôn ngữ**, không phải bài để đọc thuộc. Hãy:

- Ghi 3–5 từ khóa.
- Học 3–5 cụm từ.
- Tự nói bằng thông tin thật của bạn.
- Chỉ giữ cấu trúc, không giữ nguyên câu.

### 0.4 Rubric tự chấm B1 (1–5)

| Điểm | Fluency & Coherence | Vocabulary | Grammar | Pronunciation |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Nói gần đủ thời lượng, ý nối rõ, ít ngắt | Dùng được 8–10 cụm mới, chính xác | Ít lỗi, có câu phức | Rõ, trọng âm tốt, ít lỗi |
| 4 | Nói đủ 70% thời lượng, có vài ngắt | Dùng 6–8 cụm, đôi chỗ chọn sai | Có lỗi nhưng hiểu | Rõ, còn lỗi âm cuối |
| 3 | Nói 50–70%, ngắt do tìm từ | Dùng 4–5 cụm, lặp | Lỗi ảnh hưởng một số câu | Người nghe phải cố |
| 2 | Nói ngắn, cần gợi ý | Vốn từ hạn chế | Lỗi nhiều | Khó hiểu thường xuyên |
| 1 | Chưa trả lời được | Rất hạn chế | Không kiểm soát | Không hiểu |

## Topic 1 — Family (Part 1 Fluency)

**Mục tiêu:** Trả lời 6 câu Part 1 về gia đình bằng 2–4 câu; dùng *because, but, so*; không trả lời một từ.  
**Thời lượng:** 30 phút.

### Part 1 questions

1. How many people are there in your family?
2. Do you live with your parents?
3. Who are you closest to in your family?
4. Do you spend much time with your family?
5. What do you usually do together?
6. Is family important in your country?

### Useful language

| Cụm từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| immediate family | gia đình ruột thịt | There are four people in my immediate family. |
| extended family | đại gia đình | We often visit our extended family. |
| be close to | thân với | I'm close to my sister. |
| spend time together | dành thời gian cùng nhau | We spend time together at weekends. |
| have meals together | ăn cơm cùng nhau | We usually have dinner together. |
| support each other | hỗ trợ nhau | My family supports each other. |
| only child | con một | I'm an only child. |
| get on well with | hòa thuận với | I get on well with my parents. |
| family gathering | buổi sum họp gia đình | We have a family gathering every year. |
| take care of | chăm sóc | We take care of my grandmother. |

### Model answers

> **How many people are there in your family?**
>
> There are four people in my family: my parents, my younger sister and me. We live in a small apartment near the city centre. My sister is still at school, and I'm a university student.

> **Who are you closest to in your family?**
>
> I'm closest to my mother, I think. She's very patient and always listens to me. We often talk in the evening while we're cooking dinner. I can tell her about my problems, and she gives me practical advice.

### Pronunciation focus

- **Final /m/:** *family* /ˈfæməli/, *time* /taɪm/, *home* /həʊm/.
- **Word stress:** *immediate* /ɪˈmiːdiət/ — nhấn âm 2; *extended* /ɪkˈstendɪd/ — nhấn âm 2.
- **Linking:** *members of my family* → /ˈmembəz‿əv maɪ ˈfæməli/.

### Common Vietnamese mistakes

| ❌ Sai | ✅ Đúng | Vì sao |
|---|---|---|
| There are four people in my family, include my parents. | ... **including** my parents. | Cần giới từ/gerund. |
| I very close to my mother. | I **am very close** to my mother. | Thiếu *be*; *very* đứng trước tính từ. |
| My family have four people. | My family **has** four people. | *Family* số ít trong ngữ cảnh này. |

### Speaking task

1. Ghi 5 từ khóa về gia đình bạn.
2. Trả lời 6 câu, mỗi câu 2–4 câu.
3. Ghi âm 2 phút.
4. Nghe lại, đếm số câu và lỗi.
5. Ghi âm lại, dùng ít nhất 4 cụm trong language bank.


## Topic 2 — A Place You Like (Part 2 Planning)

**Mục tiêu:** Chuẩn bị 1 phút; nói 1,5–2 phút về một địa điểm; dùng PPF (Past–Present–Future) và 5 giác quan.  
**Thời lượng:** 35 phút.

### Cue card

> Describe a place you like to visit.
>
> You should say:
> - where it is
> - when you go there
> - what you do there
> - and explain why you like it.

### Useful language

| Cụm từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| It's located in ... | Nó nằm ở ... | It's located in the north of the city. |
| I've been going there since ... | Tôi đến đó từ ... | I've been going there since 2020. |
| What I like most is ... | Điều tôi thích nhất là ... | What I like most is the atmosphere. |
| It's within walking distance | Nó trong khoảng cách đi bộ | It's within walking distance of my house. |
| It's a great place to ... | Đó là nơi tuyệt vời để ... | It's a great place to relax. |
| I often go there with ... | Tôi thường đến đó với ... | I often go there with my friends. |
| It reminds me of ... | Nó gợi nhớ cho tôi về ... | It reminds me of my childhood. |
| Looking back, ... | Nhìn lại, ... | Looking back, I appreciate it more now. |
| It has become part of my routine | Nó đã thành thói quen | It has become part of my routine. |
| I'd recommend it to ... | Tôi sẽ giới thiệu nó cho ... | I'd recommend it to anyone who likes nature. |

### Outline

| Phần | Nội dung | Từ khóa |
|---|---|---|
| What/where | công viên gần nhà | park, near my house, 10 minutes |
| When | cuối tuần, từ 2020 | weekends, since 2020 |
| What you do | đi bộ, đọc sách, picnic | walk, read, picnic |
| Why | yên tĩnh, nhiều cây, thoải mái | quiet, trees, relax |
| Feeling | giảm stress | recharge |

### Model answer — B1

> I'd like to talk about a park near my house. It's about ten minutes away on foot, in the north of the city. I've been going there since 2020, usually at weekends with my friends or my sister.
>
> The park isn't very big, but it has a lot of trees and a small lake. We often walk around the lake, sit on a bench and talk. Sometimes we bring snacks and have a picnic. In the summer, it's a good place to read a book because it's much quieter than the city centre.
>
> I like it because it helps me relax. After a busy week, I just need some fresh air and a quiet place. It's also free, so I can go there whenever I want. I'd recommend it to anyone who wants to escape the noise of the city.

*150 từ · khoảng 1,5 phút.*

### Model answer — B1+ / B2 bridge

> I'd like to talk about a small park near my home, which has become an important part of my weekly routine. It's located in the north of the city, about ten minutes away on foot. I've been going there since 2020, usually at weekends with a couple of friends.
>
> What I like most is the atmosphere. It's not a famous park, so it's never too crowded. There are a lot of old trees, a small lake and a few benches. We usually walk around the lake, chat and sometimes have a picnic. In summer, I often take a book and read under a tree. It's much quieter than the city centre, and I can hear birds instead of traffic.
>
> Looking back, I think the park has become more important to me over time. It's a place where I can switch off and recharge after a busy week. In the future, I'd like to visit more often, maybe early in the morning before work. I'd recommend it to anyone who needs a peaceful place in the city.

*183 từ · khoảng 1,5–2 phút.*

### Pronunciation focus

- **/θ/ in *north* /nɔːθ/ and *think* /θɪŋk/**: lưỡi giữa hai răng.
- **Word stress:** *atmosphere* /ˈætməsfɪə/ — nhấn âm 1; *recommend* /ˌrekəˈmend/ — nhấn âm 3.
- **Chunking:** *What I like most / is the atmosphere.*

### Common Vietnamese mistakes

| ❌ Sai | ✅ Đúng | Vì sao |
|---|---|---|
| It's near to my house. | It's **near** my house. | *Near* không cần *to*. |
| I go there since 2020. | I **have gone** there since 2020. | *Since* dùng với present perfect. |
| It's very quiet, so I can relax. | It's very quiet, **which** helps me relax. | Câu phức tự nhiên hơn. |

### Speaking task

1. Ghi 5 từ khóa trong 1 phút.
2. Nói 1,5 phút, không dừng.
3. Nghe lại, đánh dấu chỗ ngắt.
4. Nói lại, thêm 1 chi tiết về cảm giác.
5. Ghi âm lần cuối, so với model.

### AI feedback prompt

```text

Cue card: [dán cue card]
Transcript: [dán transcript]
Thời lượng: [phút]
Hãy:
1. Chấm 1-5 cho 4 tiêu chí.
2. Kiểm tra tôi có trả lời đủ 4 ý trong cue card không.
3. Chỉ ra 3 chỗ có thể mở rộng bằng chi tiết/giác quan.
4. Đề xuất 5 cụm từ hay hơn.
5. Viết 1 câu mở đầu và 1 câu kết mẫu.
```

## Topic 3 — Technology (Part 3 Explanation)

**Mục tiêu:** Trả lời Part 3 có quan điểm, lý do, ví dụ, concession; dùng ngôn ngữ dự đoán.  
**Thời lượng:** 35 phút.

### Part 3 questions

1. How has technology changed the way people communicate?
2. Do you think people spend too much time on their phones?
3. What are the advantages and disadvantages of online learning?
4. Will artificial intelligence replace some jobs in the future?
5. How can older people learn to use new technology?
6. Should schools teach children about online safety?

### Useful language

| Cụm từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| One major change is ... | Một thay đổi lớn là ... | One major change is the speed of communication. |
| It has made it easier to ... | Nó giúp ... dễ hơn | It has made it easier to stay in touch. |
| On the one hand, ... | Một mặt, ... | On the one hand, phones are convenient. |
| On the other hand, ... | Mặt khác, ... | On the other hand, they can be distracting. |
| It's likely that ... | Có khả năng là ... | It's likely that AI will change many jobs. |
| I doubt that ... | Tôi nghi ngờ rằng ... | I doubt that AI will replace all teachers. |
| For example, ... | Ví dụ, ... | For example, many students use apps. |
| However, it's also true that ... | Tuy nhiên, cũng đúng là ... | However, it's also true that ... |
| On balance, ... | Nhìn chung, ... | On balance, I think ... |
| It depends on ... | Nó phụ thuộc vào ... | It depends on how people use it. |

### Model answers

> **Do you think people spend too much time on their phones?**
>
> Yes, I think many people do, including me sometimes. Smartphones are designed to keep us looking at the screen, so it's easy to lose track of time. For example, I often check my phone for a few minutes and then realise an hour has passed. However, I don't think the phone itself is the problem. It depends on how we use it. If we use it for work, learning or keeping in touch, it can be useful. The problem is when it replaces sleep, exercise or face-to-face conversation.

> **Will artificial intelligence replace some jobs in the future?**
>
> I think it will replace some tasks, but not entire jobs in most cases. AI is very good at repetitive work, such as checking data or answering simple questions. For example, some companies already use chatbots for customer service. However, it's also true that many jobs require judgement, creativity and human contact. A doctor, for instance, needs to understand a patient's feelings, not just read test results. On balance, I believe AI will change jobs more than it will eliminate them.

### Pronunciation focus

- **/ʃ/ and /tʃ/:** *machine* /məˈʃiːn/, *change* /tʃeɪndʒ/.
- **Word stress:** *technology* /tekˈnɒlədʒi/ — nhấn âm 2; *artificial* /ˌɑːtɪˈfɪʃl/ — nhấn âm 3; *intelligence* /ɪnˈtelɪdʒəns/ — nhấn âm 2.
- **Intonation:** concession *However, ...* — xuống giọng nhẹ, rồi tiếp tục.

### Common Vietnamese mistakes

| ❌ Sai | ✅ Đúng | Vì sao |
|---|---|---|
| Technology helps us to communicate more easier. | ... **more easily**. | Cần trạng từ. |
| It is depend on how we use it. | It **depends** on how we use it. | *Depend* là động từ. |
| I think it will replace all jobs, but I am not sure. | I think it **may** replace some jobs, but I'm not sure. | Tránh tuyệt đối hóa. |

### Speaking task

1. Chọn 3 câu Part 3.
2. Trả lời mỗi câu 4–6 câu, có lý do + ví dụ.
3. Ghi âm 3 phút.
4. Nghe lại, đánh dấu câu trả lời thiếu ví dụ.
5. Nói lại, thêm 1 concession.

### AI feedback prompt

```text

2. Chỉ ra chỗ thiếu lý do/ví dụ.
3. Đề xuất 5 cụm B1+ để diễn đạt quan điểm.
4. Sửa 3 lỗi ngữ pháp quan trọng.

```

## Topic 4 — Work & Study

**Mục tiêu:** Nói về công việc/học tập; so sánh; giải thích lựa chọn; dùng present perfect.  
**Thời lượng:** 30 phút.

### Part 1 questions

1. Do you work or are you a student?
2. What do you study?
3. Why did you choose this subject?
4. What do you like most about your work/study?
5. What is difficult about it?
6. What would you like to do in the future?

### Useful language

| Cụm từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| major in | học chuyên ngành | I major in business. |
| be responsible for | chịu trách nhiệm | I'm responsible for customer support. |
| deal with | xử lý | I deal with complaints. |
| gain experience | tích lũy kinh nghiệm | I want to gain experience. |
| career path | con đường sự nghiệp | I'm still choosing my career path. |
| work under pressure | làm việc dưới áp lực | I can work under pressure. |
| practical skills | kỹ năng thực tế | The course teaches practical skills. |
| theoretical | lý thuyết | Some subjects are very theoretical. |

### Part 2 cue card

> Describe a job you would like to do.
>
> You should say:
> - what the job is
> - what skills it requires
> - why you are interested in it
> - and explain how you would prepare for it.

### Outline

| Phần | Nội dung |
|---|---|
| Job | tour guide |
| Skills | English, communication, local knowledge |
| Why | meet people, travel, share culture |
| Preparation | improve English, get certificate, practise |

### Model answer

> I'd like to talk about a job I would like to do in the future: a tour guide. I've always enjoyed travelling and meeting people, and I think this job would combine both.
>
> A tour guide needs several skills. First, you need to speak English clearly, because many tourists come from other countries. You also need good communication skills and a lot of local knowledge. For example, you should know the history of the places you visit and be able to answer questions. You also need to be patient and friendly, because tourists may be tired or worried.
>
> I'm interested in this job because I like sharing information about my city. I also enjoy learning from people from different cultures. In the future, I plan to improve my English, take a tour guide course and practise by showing my friends around. I think it would be a challenging but enjoyable job.

*154 từ · khoảng 1,5 phút.*

### Part 3 questions

1. Is it better to choose a job you enjoy or a job with a high salary?
2. Why do some people change jobs frequently?
3. How can companies help employees learn new skills?

### Model answer

> **Is it better to choose a job you enjoy or a job with a high salary?**
>
> I think it depends on the person's situation. If someone has a family and needs to pay for housing and education, a high salary may be more important. However, in the long term, I believe job satisfaction matters more. People spend a large part of their lives at work, and if they hate their job, they may feel stressed and unhappy. For example, someone who loves teaching may accept a lower salary because the work gives them meaning. On balance, I'd choose a job I enjoy, as long as it pays enough to live comfortably.

### Pronunciation focus

- **/ɜː/:** *work* /wɜːk/, *learn* /lɜːn/, *research* /rɪˈsɜːtʃ/.
- **Word stress:** *responsible* /rɪˈspɒnsəbl/ — nhấn âm 2; *experience* /ɪkˈspɪəriəns/ — nhấn âm 2.
- **Final clusters:** *skills* /skɪlz/, *tasks* /tɑːsks/.

### Common Vietnamese mistakes

| ❌ Sai | ✅ Đúng | Vì sao |
|---|---|---|
| I'm working in a company since 2022. | I'**ve worked** in a company since 2022. | *Since* + present perfect. |
| I want to improve my English skills more. | I want to **improve my English**. | *Skills* thường không cần *more* ở đây. |
| My job is very boring, I want to change. | My job is very boring, **so** I want to change. | Cần liên từ. |

### Speaking task

1. Trả lời 6 câu Part 1.
2. Chuẩn bị cue card 1 phút, nói 1,5 phút.
3. Trả lời 3 câu Part 3.
4. Ghi âm, nghe lại, ghi 2 lỗi.

## Topic 5 — Hometown

**Mục tiêu:** Mô tả nơi sống; so sánh quá khứ–hiện tại; dùng *used to*, *there is/are*, comparatives.  
**Thời lượng:** 30 phút.

### Part 1 questions

1. Where is your hometown?
2. What is it famous for?
3. Do you like living there?
4. Has it changed in recent years?
5. What do people do there for fun?
6. Would you like to live there in the future?

### Useful language

| Cụm từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| be famous for | nổi tiếng về | It's famous for its beaches. |
| used to | từng | It used to be a quiet village. |
| It has changed a lot | Nó đã thay đổi nhiều | The city has changed a lot. |
| peaceful | yên bình | It's peaceful and friendly. |
| crowded | đông đúc | It gets crowded in summer. |
| local food | món ăn địa phương | The local food is amazing. |
| within reach | trong tầm với | The beach is within reach. |
| sense of community | cảm giác cộng đồng | There's a strong sense of community. |

### Part 2 cue card

> Describe your hometown.
>
> You should say:
> - where it is
> - what it is like
> - what you like about it
> - and explain how it has changed.

### Model answer

> My hometown is a small city in central Vietnam, near the coast. It's famous for its long beach and seafood. I lived there until I was eighteen, and I still go back during holidays.
>
> It's a fairly peaceful place. The streets are not as busy as in a big city, and people know their neighbours. There are a few markets, a river and a lot of small cafés. In the evening, many people walk along the beach or sit outside and talk. The local food is excellent, especially the fish and noodles.
>
> The city has changed a lot in recent years. It used to be much quieter, but now there are more hotels and tourists. Some old buildings have been replaced by new shops. I like the development because it brings jobs, but I also miss the quiet atmosphere. In the future, I'd like to live there again if I can find good work.

> Social media has made news much faster and more accessible. People can see updates on their phones within minutes, and they can share stories with friends. However, this speed can also cause problems. False or misleading information can spread quickly, and people may only see news that matches their own opinions. For example, a shocking headline may be shared thousands of times before it is checked. I think people need to compare different sources and not believe everything they see online.
