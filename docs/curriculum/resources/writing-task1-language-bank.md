# Writing Task 1 Language Bank — IELTS Academic

> Dùng cho B1–C2. Mục tiêu: có đủ ngôn ngữ để mô tả 7 dạng bài mà không học thuộc template.

---

## 1. Cấu trúc chuẩn

1. **Introduction** — paraphrase đề.
2. **Overview** — 1–2 câu nêu xu hướng/khác biệt chính.
3. **Body 1** — nhóm dữ liệu thứ nhất.
4. **Body 2** — nhóm dữ liệu thứ hai.

Không cần kết luận riêng. Không đưa ý kiến cá nhân.

---

## 2. Paraphrase introduction

### Cấu trúc

- `The chart shows ...`
- `The graph illustrates ...`
- `The table compares ...`
- `The diagram describes ...`
- `The maps show ...`

### Thay đổi từ

| Đề bài | Paraphrase |
|---|---|
| show | illustrate, compare, give information about, present |
| number of | the figure for, the quantity of, how many |
| percentage of | the proportion of, the share of |
| from 2010 to 2020 | between 2010 and 2020, over a ten-year period |
| in three countries | across three countries |
| spending on | expenditure on |
| people who | the number of people who |

### Ví dụ

> Đề: The chart shows the percentage of households with internet access in four countries between 2000 and 2020.

> Intro: The line graph compares the proportion of households with internet access in four countries over a twenty-year period from 2000 to 2020.

---

## 3. Overview language

Overview phải nêu **xu hướng chung**, không liệt kê số liệu.

### Tăng / giảm

- Overall, the figure for X **rose steadily**.
- Overall, X **saw a significant increase**.
- Overall, the number of X **declined gradually**.
- Overall, X **remained stable**.
- Overall, X **fluctuated** throughout the period.

### So sánh

- X was **consistently higher than** Y.
- X **overtook** Y in 2015.
- X and Y **followed a similar pattern**.
- The gap between X and Y **narrowed/widened**.
- X was **the most popular** category, while Y was **the least common**.

### Dạng không có thời gian

- Overall, **more men than women** chose X.
- Overall, X accounted for **the largest share**.
- Overall, the figures for X and Y were **broadly similar**.

### Lỗi cần tránh

- Không đưa số liệu cụ thể trong overview.
- Không giải thích nguyên nhân.
- Không dùng `I think`, `maybe`, `perhaps` quá nhiều.

## 4. Line graph

### 4.1 Động từ mô tả xu hướng

| Tăng | Giảm | Ổn định | Dao động |
|---|---|---|---|
| rise | fall | remain stable | fluctuate |
| increase | decrease | stay constant | vary |
| grow | decline | level off | oscillate |
| climb | drop | plateau | |
| surge | plunge | | |
| double | halve | | |

### 4.2 Danh từ

- a rise, an increase, a growth
- a fall, a decrease, a decline, a drop
- a peak, a low
- a fluctuation
- a plateau

### 4.3 Trạng từ / tính từ

| Trạng từ | Tính từ |
|---|---|
| sharply | sharp |
| significantly | significant |
| steadily | steady |
| gradually | gradual |
| slightly | slight |
| dramatically | dramatic |
| marginally | marginal |

### 4.4 Cấu trúc

- `X rose sharply from 10% to 25%.`
- `There was a sharp rise in X from 10% to 25%.`
- `X experienced a sharp rise, from 10% to 25%.`
- `X peaked at 40% in 2010.`
- `X hit a low of 5% in 2005.`
- `X remained stable at around 20% between 2010 and 2015.`
- `X fluctuated between 15% and 25%.`
- `X overtook Y in 2012.`
- `The gap between X and Y narrowed.`

### 4.5 Giới từ

- `rose to 20%` — tăng đến
- `rose by 5%` — tăng thêm 5%
- `fell from 20% to 10%`
- `peaked at 30%`
- `remained at 15%`
- `between 2010 and 2020`
- `over the period`

### 4.6 Đoạn mẫu

> The line graph compares the percentage of people using three types of transport in a city between 2000 and 2020. Overall, car use declined steadily, while cycling and public transport became more popular. Car use was the highest in 2000, at 60%, but it fell to 35% by 2020. By contrast, cycling rose from 10% to 25%, and public transport increased from 20% to 30%. The gap between car use and the other two modes narrowed significantly over the period.

## 5. Bar chart

### 5.1 Ngôn ngữ so sánh

- `X was higher than Y.`
- `X was slightly higher than Y.`
- `X was nearly twice as high as Y.`
- `X was the highest, at 40%.`
- `X was the lowest, at 10%.`
- `X ranked first/second.`
- `X accounted for the largest proportion.`
- `The figure for X was similar to that for Y.`

### 5.2 Nhóm dữ liệu

- `The categories can be divided into two groups: ...`
- `The highest figures were seen in ...`
- `The lowest figures were recorded for ...`
- `There was a clear difference between ... and ...`

### 5.3 Đoạn mẫu

> The bar chart compares the amount of time spent on four leisure activities by men and women. Overall, watching TV was the most popular activity for both groups, while reading was the least common. Men spent 20 hours a week watching TV, compared with 18 hours for women. Women spent slightly more time reading than men, at 6 and 4 hours respectively. The figures for exercise were similar, at around 5 hours for both groups.

## 6. Pie chart

### 6.1 Ngôn ngữ tỉ lệ

- `accounted for 40% of the total`
- `made up one third of ...`
- `represented a quarter of ...`
- `constituted the largest share`
- `was the smallest category, at 5%`
- `the remaining 20%`
- `just under/over half`
- `approximately two thirds`

### 6.2 Phân số

| Phần | Cách viết |
|---|---|
| 50% | half / one in two |
| 25% | a quarter / one in four |
| 33% | a third |
| 20% | a fifth |
| 75% | three quarters |
| 10% | one in ten |

### 6.3 Đoạn mẫu

> The pie charts show how a household spent its monthly budget in 2010 and 2020. Overall, housing remained the largest expense, while spending on food fell. In 2010, housing accounted for 40% of the total, followed by food at 25%. By 2020, housing had risen to 45%, whereas the share of food had dropped to 15%. Transport made up 20% in both years, and the remaining categories changed only slightly.

## 7. Table

### 7.1 Cách xử lý

- Không mô tả từng ô.
- Tìm **hàng/cột nổi bật**: cao nhất, thấp nhất, tăng mạnh, ổn định.
- Nhóm theo xu hướng hoặc theo mức.
- So sánh chéo nếu có nhiều đối tượng.

### 7.2 Ngôn ngữ

- `The table compares ...`
- `The most striking feature is ...`
- `X had the highest figure for ...`
- `In contrast, Y recorded the lowest ...`
- `The figures for X and Y were almost identical.`
- `X was three times higher than Y.`

### 7.3 Đoạn mẫu

> The table shows the average monthly salary and working hours in four industries. Overall, employees in the technology sector earned the most and worked the longest hours, while those in retail earned the least. Technology workers earned $4,000 a month, compared with $2,500 in education and $2,000 in retail. Working hours followed a similar pattern: technology staff worked 45 hours a week, whereas retail staff worked 35. The figures for healthcare were in between.

## 8. Process

### 8.1 Cấu trúc

- Câu bị động: `is heated`, `are mixed`, `is transported`.
- Sequencers: `First`, `Next`, `After that`, `Then`, `Finally`.
- Mục đích: `in order to`, `so that`, `to`.

### 8.2 Ngôn ngữ

- `The diagram illustrates the process by which ...`
- `The process consists of ... stages.`
- `First, ... is collected.`
- `It is then transported to ...`
- `After being heated, ...`
- `Once the mixture has cooled, it is ...`
- `Finally, ... is packaged and delivered.`

### 8.3 Đoạn mẫu

> The diagram shows how coffee is produced, from the farm to the consumer. The process consists of six main stages. First, coffee beans are harvested by hand and then dried in the sun. After that, the beans are roasted at a high temperature and ground into powder. Next, the powder is mixed with hot water to make coffee. Finally, the coffee is packaged and delivered to shops.

## 9. Map

### 9.1 Ngôn ngữ vị trí

- `in the north/south/east/west of`
- `to the north of`
- `in the centre of`
- `along the river`
- `next to`, `opposite`, `behind`, `in front of`
- `on the corner of`

### 9.2 Thay đổi

- `was replaced by`
- `was demolished`
- `was converted into`
- `was extended`
- `was built`
- `was removed`
- `remained unchanged`
- `was relocated to`

### 9.3 Đoạn mẫu

> The maps show the changes to a coastal town between 2000 and 2020. Overall, the town became more developed, with new housing and tourist facilities. In 2000, the area to the north of the river was mainly farmland. By 2020, this land had been replaced by a hotel and a shopping centre. The old fishing harbour in the south remained unchanged, but a new road was built along the coast. The park in the centre was extended, and a car park was added next to it.

## 10. Mixed charts

### 10.1 Cách xử lý

- Mỗi biểu đồ có thể là một body.
- Tìm **mối liên hệ** giữa hai biểu đồ.
- Overview nêu đặc điểm chung của cả hai.

### 10.2 Ngôn ngữ liên kết

- `The first chart shows ..., while the second illustrates ...`
- `A similar pattern can be seen in ...`
- `By contrast, ...`
- `Taken together, the two charts suggest that ...`
- `The figures in the second chart support this trend.`

### 10.3 Đoạn mẫu

> The bar chart shows the number of visitors to three museums, and the pie chart shows how visitors rated their experience. Overall, the most visited museum also received the highest satisfaction rating. The National Museum attracted 50,000 visitors, compared with 30,000 for the City Museum and 20,000 for the History Museum. In the pie chart, 60% of visitors rated their experience as excellent, while only 10% said it was poor.

## 11. So sánh và dự đoán

### So sánh

- `X was twice as high as Y.`
- `X was slightly lower than Y.`
- `X was the same as Y.`
- `X was similar to Y, at around 20%.`
- `X was the second most popular choice.`

### Xấp xỉ

- `approximately`, `around`, `roughly`, `about`
- `just over`, `just under`
- `nearly`, `almost`

### Không chắc chắn

- `This may be because ...` — chỉ dùng nếu đề cho phép giải thích; Task 1 thường không cần.
- Tránh: `I think`, `maybe`, `perhaps` trong bài Task 1.

---

## 12. Câu phức hữu ích

- `While X rose, Y fell.`
- `Although X was the most popular, it declined over the period.`
- `X was higher than Y, whereas Z remained stable.`
- `The figure for X, which was 20% in 2010, rose to 30% in 2020.`
- `With the exception of X, all categories increased.`
- `X was the only category that did not change.`

---

## 13. Lỗi thường gặp

| Lỗi | Sửa |
|---|---|
| `The graph shows about...` | `The graph shows ...` (không dùng about trong intro) |
| `It increased 20%` | `It increased by 20%` hoặc `to 20%` |
| `The percentage was risen` | `The percentage rose` |
| `More higher` | `higher` |
| `The number of percentage` | `the percentage` / `the number of people` |
| Liệt kê mọi số liệu | Chọn số liệu nổi bật, nhóm lại |
| Không có overview | Luôn có 1–2 câu overview |
| Đưa ý kiến | Không dùng `I think` |
| Sai thì | Dùng quá khứ cho dữ liệu quá khứ; hiện tại cho biểu đồ không thời gian |

---

## 14. Checklist trước khi nộp

- [ ] Intro paraphrase, không copy đề.
- [ ] Overview rõ, không số liệu vụn.
- [ ] Body có nhóm dữ liệu hợp lý.
- [ ] Có so sánh, không chỉ liệt kê.
- [ ] Đúng đơn vị, năm, %.
- [ ] Đủ 150 từ.
- [ ] Không đưa ý kiến/nguyên nhân ngoài biểu đồ.
- [ ] Kiểm tra mạo từ, số ít/số nhiều, thì.
