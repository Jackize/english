Adjective /əˈfem(ə)rəl/

Ephemeral

menu_book The Core Meaning

Lasting for a very short time; transient. Often used to describe things that are beautiful but fleeting, like a sunset or a delicate flower.

hub Words with Similar Spirit

  • Fleeting
  • Transient
  • Short-lived

visibility Seeing it in Action

"He realized that internet fame is often ephemeral, gone as quickly as it arrived."
